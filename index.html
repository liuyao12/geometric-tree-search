complete A₂-sliced census

A complete long run separates two tilers from six harder cases

We enumerated every connected union of 9 and 10 consecutive-layer A₂ alcoves in the chosen rigid-lattice model, then used exact translational-quotient screens to remove the easy periodic cases. The size-10 pass alone checked 98,537 tiles; a deeper six-copy probe left 13 of its 72 ranked classes alive, each with a replayed first corona. A longer determinant-12 campaign then exhausted all 455 eight-copy quotients for the eight recovered size-9 leads: two have exact periodic tilings, while six survive every quotient in that complete screen.

This is a search success, not an aperiodicity claim: the survivors are hard benchmark cases whose larger periodic domains—or failure to tile—remain unresolved. Each positive 8-tile quotient also carries a replayed scale-2 cluster substitution.

What GCTS Studies

GCTS sits near constraint programming, exact cover, tiling search, and geometric deep learning, but emphasizes the growing search tree itself: frontier choices, symmetry classes of moves, reusable patches, and checkable local certificates.
