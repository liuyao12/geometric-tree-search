# Conditional attachment experiment, Notebook 65

This is a specialized finite point-model study. Every original command,
guard and factored lemma-input placement remains present. The target roots
have generation zero and unit occupancy. The ordinary complete frontier
graph checks global dead ends, then forced moves, then generation before
candidate count. No clause scanner runs during a marked search.

The learner fixes two complete canonical formula words in an original
prefix. Every root and candidate in that prefix is retained under a verified
decorated-candidate lift from the full declaration. All references point to
strictly earlier rows. Therefore a full proof containing those two words
would restrict to a teacher completion. Exhaustion justifies excluding the
conjunction in this exact theory, vocabulary and registered inventory.
Cutoffs are unknown and authorize no assignment.

Each negative gets a fresh marking-only point. Left-only writers assign
zero and right-only writers assign one. A candidate writing both forbidden
words gets a different point with fixed boundary zero and tile value one.
All original formula words include their distinct terminator. The combined
layer is the compatible union with independently certified unary markings.

## Interpretation

This is one paired observation per layer and ordering, with explicitly
reused families and frozen policy. Collection times include immediate
teacher replay; the subsequent complete audit, model construction, native
admission and proof checks are additional costs. Attempted placement counts
are not total-cost speedups. Inventory amortization and learned-policy
generalization require separate controlled benchmarks.

The declarations and formula bounds are inputs; no proof sequence is supplied.
Neither an exhausted finite envelope nor a teacher negative proves an
unrestricted mathematical statement unprovable. This study does not implement
the Turtle pair-corona protocol, full first-order search, PA induction,
Hilbert axioms, symmetry transfer or infinite tiling coverage.
