How a symbol becomes a lattice tile

Neighbouring half-points carry grammar states that must agree. End caps fill the two outer half-points. All strip points start at the same age. GCTS can place a symbol in the middle while both ends are still empty.

A learned mark records an ordered pair of symbols at a fixed offset. For each excluded pair, its left symbol writes one value at that offset and its right symbol writes a different value at its own centre. When both marks land on the same point, the placement is excluded. Marks may reach beyond the tile’s three occupied points.

The learner excludes pairs observed at least three times in failed rows and never in a winning training row, at distances of two through seven cells. Differentiation makes the final decision. The replay shows every placement and rollback at the concluding width. Totals include all shorter widths and fallback passes.
