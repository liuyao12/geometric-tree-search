Move rules, search cost and limits

The game uses linearity, the product rule and the elementary derivatives of its four factors. Primitives are considered up to an additive constant on the real line. The catalogue enumerates every nonconstant monomial in those factors with a nonzero rational coefficient. Products may repeat factors.

Each extraction costs one plus its number of factors, plus one for each extra unit in the reduced numerator magnitude or denominator. Both signs have the same cost. A stored proof costs one move when a proportional copy of its integrand occurs as a group of terms. Its derivative is checked on admission. Search merges identical expanded remainders. Within each cost GCTS orders the candidate moves at one open rewrite obligation.

The interactive search stops after cost seven; an open problem at that limit can still have a proof. The hand controls accept coefficients up to 99 in magnitude and up to twelve factors per move.
