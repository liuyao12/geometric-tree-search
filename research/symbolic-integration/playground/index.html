What is supplied, and what is learned?

The experiment supplies the substitution, a choice of three elementary functions per degree, and their symbolic derivatives. GCTS chooses tiles and solves for their coefficients. A nonzero derivative residual rejects the row. SymPy checked the original-variable answers in the research benchmark. This player recomputes the search in your browser using exact integer coefficient checks on the supplied derivative tiles.

Training records the highest tile and adjacent tile pairs from successful rows. If those markings rule out every solution, GCTS retries without them using the remaining allowance of 512 placements.
