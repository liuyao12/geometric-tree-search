White marks are different interior points chosen separately in each reflected tile. All 1,009 marks in this patch stay fixed throughout the plane-view animation.

One fixed tile, fourteen fixed coordinate labels. Coordinates are relative to the first vertex. Click a tile in the patch to inspect that tile here.

The integer tuple \( (p,q,r,s) \) uses two triangular lattice bases. It is not a tuple of distances along four perpendicular axes.

How these coordinates produce the drawing

Each tuple represents the Cartesian point

\[ (u_x,u_y,v_x,v_y)=\left(p+\frac q2,\frac{\sqrt3 q}{2},\frac{\sqrt3 r}{2},\frac r2+s\right). \]

The moving plane uses \( \cos\theta\,u+\sin\theta\,v \), from \( \theta=\pi/3 \) for the hat to \( \theta=\pi/6 \) for the turtle. The coordinate labels stay fixed.

The object stays fixed.

Every corner has a fixed position in four dimensions. This animation changes the viewing plane. The entire patch moves together, so shared corners and edges remain shared.

Use Plane view to see the tiling. Lifted view adds a third projected direction and tilts the camera, making the folded patch visible. Overlaps in that view are an effect of the projection.

Fixed interior marks.

Each reflected copy has its own fixed point on a triangulated tile surface in four dimensions. One shared camera rotation, scaling and translation makes every white mark stationary in Plane view. The marks are strictly inside their tiles throughout the motion.

This has an exact certificate for all 1,009 reflected tiles in the displayed patch. It does not claim a proof for an infinite tiling. The construction and verification explain the choice of points.

A steadier reflected layout.

Steady reflected layout applies one rotation, uniform scaling and translation to the entire patch. It matches the dark tiles’ overall arrangement to its hat view. No tile is moved separately, so shared edges still agree. Small local shifts remain; this does not freeze every dark tile exactly.

Follow the orange outline.

The outlined tile is the same tile throughout. Colors stay attached to individual tiles. Dark blue tiles are reflected copies; the remaining colors distinguish groups in the construction.

At the equal-edge midpoint, the polygon also admits periodic tilings. The particular patch shown here continues smoothly through that point.
