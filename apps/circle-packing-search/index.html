What this page decides

A precise search inside a deliberately restricted grammar.

A green result is a directly checkable packing certificate: interiors are disjoint, every requested bend occurs, and every small circle has at least three tangencies that do not all lie on one side of a diameter. The outer boundary counts as one contact.

An exhausted result only rules out configurations generated by these corner insertions up to the chosen circle horizon. It is not a proof that no unrestricted packing exists.
