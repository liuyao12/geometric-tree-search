What this pilot establishes

A successful movie proves equivalence by Reidemeister replay. Different Fox coloring dimensions prove inequivalence. Exhausting the crossing and time bounds leaves general knot equivalence unknown. All legal base candidates remain available to the RL lane.

The current tiles carry entire diagram seams. Failure labels come from exhaustive bounded reachability and become ordinary point-marking mismatches. This is a specialized seam control, with the same point solver in every lane. Local hexagonal marking synthesis, spatial realization of every movie frame, and a full classifier remain research goals.
