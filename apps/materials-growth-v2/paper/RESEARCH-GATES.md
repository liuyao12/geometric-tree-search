# Research gates before a high-impact materials-methods submission

This is a research plan, not a statement of journal suitability or acceptance.
Nature's criteria include originality, scientific importance and broad interest:
https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/editorial-criteria-and-processes

## Current evidence

- Follow-up diagnosis: all 14 absent reference sites in the 220-atom ice
  checkpoint were pending frontier obligations; two remain pending at 440 atoms.
  Do not infer missing rules from that finite-prefix coverage deficit alone.
- A finite candidate-conditioned compiler now exactly encodes forbidden pairs as
  point markings, with exact redundancy/star reductions and exhaustive controls.
  It is a verification reference, not a reusable motif-local spatial field.

- A same-result implementation ablation reduces redundant validity evaluations.
- Eight deterministic structural-sensitivity runs add coverage, neighbor recovery,
  composition and angular diagnostics. They do not show an overlap-rule advantage.
- The reference cell is evaluation-only. There is no held-out amorphous ensemble
  experiment, executed state-of-the-art materials comparator, or physical validation.
- The current relational overlap filter is not a verified compilation into general
  point-value GCTS markings. Completion of that representation is a method gate.

## Gate 1: model and verification

Specify the point domain, candidate universe, approximate-registration semantics,
coverage target, boundary conditions and meaning of a missing observation. Verify
the learned relation-to-marking representation, including rotations and loops.
Preserve the production baseline and an independent reference verifier. Do not
replace an unknown result by a success label to obtain a favorable benchmark.

## Gate 2: reconstruction and failure mechanisms

Use the eight-run study as a diagnostic, not a dataset for choosing the best
tolerance and reporting it as an independent result. Investigate missing sites
and composition bias at matched atom counts AND matched spatial windows. Separate
frontier incompleteness from interior defects. Check whether registration, motif
selection, scheduler bias or the admissibility rules cause each failure. Freeze
fixes before evaluating new cases; retain the present failure records.

## Gate 3: an independent amorphous ensemble

Choose one provenance- and license-verified reference ensemble, initially a-Si or
silica. Split entire independent configurations/trajectories before motif learning.
Match preparation conditions, density and composition; do not treat correlated
frames as independent replicates. Vary training-set size and test unseen samples.
Define positive overlap examples and clearly scoped geometric corruptions; absence
from a finite training set is not physical negative evidence.

## Gate 4: matched baselines and meaningful outcomes

Execute the same proposal/search model without learned constraints, with extracted
relations, and with verified markings. Add an appropriate published materials
generator or reconstruction method with reproducible implementation and comparable
inputs; do not substitute a deliberately weak implementation for a field baseline.
Report structural fidelity, coverage, density, composition, partial RDFs, angles,
network statistics, structure factors, diversity, duplication and all failures.
Report time and memory per accepted configuration, training and amortization costs,
and any physical relaxation cost. No general MD speedup claim from operation counts.

## Gate 5: a central result

A candidate claim is improved data efficiency or controllable, inspectable assembly
at matched fidelity, not merely faster bookkeeping. Independent runs must support
the claim with appropriate uncertainty. A theoretical contribution instead needs
explicit assumptions and a substantive result about representation, compatibility,
extension or sampling—not a reformulation alone. Either direction should explain
why the result matters beyond the particular demonstration material.

## Gate 6: external validation and submission

For application claims, independently assess relaxation, stability and at least one
relevant property. Geometry-only generation does not forbid physics-based evaluation.
Arrange expert review, independently reproduce key findings, establish authorship,
data/code availability and limitations, and adapt the manuscript to a suitable
venue. No submission, authorship assignment or external correspondence is authorized
by this plan. A successful gate sequence would justify considering a submission;
it cannot guarantee journal acceptance.
