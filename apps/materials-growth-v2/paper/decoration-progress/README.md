# Complete-decoration alternatives: preliminary result

Three developmental experiments use the same 88 source-order evaluation frames
(44 alpha and 44 beta oxalic acid). They are not independent trajectories, and
identical thermodynamic conditions have not been established.

- Original poses: 6 verified, 82 exhausted restricted pools.
- Expanded rigid registrations: 9 verified, 1 budget-unknown, 78 exhausted.
- Original plus bounded-fit decorations: 10 verified, 1 budget-unknown, 77 exhausted.

All positives are beta. Beta-0135 is the added union success. All previous
successes survive. The three `*-check.json` files are independent replay
receipts; `results.json` binds their search and pool hashes and supplies the
interactive matrix. It contains no atom coordinates.

The unchanged point-search kernel checks global dead ends, then forced moves,
then earliest generation. Atom roots are supplied. Generated latent sites
activate when selected positive supports touch them. Integer sixths represent
t-values; interval marking checks are followed by independent Euclidean-ball
verification. Positive witnesses pass rigid fits, all active rational sums,
marking coincidence and constructive periodic reachability. Geometry remains
approximate and the candidate domain finite, not continuously exhaustive.

Bounded fitting minimizes the maximum training residual of shared off-atom
anchors and transported vector markings, retaining the existing 0.30 Å pair
tolerances and displacement-target representation prior. It produces 79
accepted group merges versus two with least squares. New complete contexts
receive newly inferred point partitions and rational weights. All 4,774
training equations pass independent verification, but 4,576 free parameters
remain. Minimum norm and minimum point count are explicit priors.

Compression alone worsens alpha transfer. The union therefore retains both
complete decoration libraries without splicing their ports or discarding old
fits. All 107,491 original candidates, 49 consistency survivors and 1,775,258
contacts are preserved by an explicit inclusion check. The union searches ten
complete positive pools; beta-0163 exceeds the 100,000-alignment preparation
budget. All 87 complete pools are independently re-enumerated.

This is not a speedup or blind growth result. Beta-0159 takes 88 backtracks in
the expanded ordering. Known target atoms, motif partitions, cell and a finite
catalog of atom-valid registrations remain given. General family transfer,
fully joint motif/anchor/t/m learning and growth beyond targets are unfinished.
The older interactive baseline is deliberately preserved below this figure.
