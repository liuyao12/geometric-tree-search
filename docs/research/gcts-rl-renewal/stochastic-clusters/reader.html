NOTEBOOK 101 · ENGINE-GENERATED TRAJECTORIES

Eight episodes produce a frozen learned policy.

Each training episode retains \(500\) attempts, \(10\) search seconds and a \(30\)-second complete worker lifecycle. At each eligible branch, a proposal is sampled from \(p_i=0.9q_i+0.1/n\), where \(q\) is the softmax of the sparse feature scores. The update uses the mixture’s actual log-gradient, including its \(0.9q_i/p_i\) correction. The episode mean gradient is multiplied by observed return minus an exponential baseline, then clipped in norm. Proposal counts never replace complete base domains.

The return rewards peak satisfied obligations and subtracts observed dead ends, backtracking, branching and proposal effort. Unknown episodes contribute their observed finite return; they are not evidence of unprovability. All rewards, corrected gradients, advantage clipping, baseline changes and weights are independently recomputed. All \(21\) numerical finite-difference checks pass; eight corruptions of the actual pilot trace are rejected.
