The directed seams inferred from the first three Golden Hex levels certify the recovered coordinate rule at every level. Two symbolic edge balances establish coverage of the recursive patches. Boundary markings become contracts for clusters

A cluster carries a short description of its boundary. The description specifies a side role, a word over \(\{0,1\}\), and a lattice placement. Each digit contributes a small directed edge path chosen by its three-digit neighborhood and its distance to the ends. The seed data have 18 role-and-context labels. Orienting the edges and assigning shared corner edges to one side yields 32 directed templates. The larger clusters inherit these same templates.

From two boundary identities to the plane

1. Reduce the word recurrence to two reusable blocks

Let \(s_0=0\), \(s_1=001\) and \(s_{k+1}=s_k s_{k-1}\), where juxtaposition concatenates words. Let \(p_k\) be \(s_k\) with its last two digits removed. The last pairs alternate between \(01\) and \(10\). The words \(s_k s_{k-1}\) and \(s_{k-1}s_k\) agree up to their last two digits. This holds for \(k=1\), and factoring out \(s_{k-1}\) reduces the next check to the preceding pair. After deletion of the last pair this gives two equal expansions. They reverse one another when the two smaller \(p\) words are palindromes, so induction from \(p_1=0\) and \(p_2=00\) proves that all \(p_k\) are palindromes.

For a level \(n\ge 4\), set \(U=p_{2n-3}\), \(V=p_{2n-2}\) and \(W=p_{2n-1}\). The checker may choose between equivalent decompositions.

\[ W=V\,10\,U=U\,01\,V,\qquad p_{2n}=W\,01\,V=V\,10\,W,\qquad p_{2n+1}=p_{2n}\,10\,W=W\,01\,p_{2n}. \]

Prefix, suffix, and forbidden-factor checks run on a finite summary of the recurrence. The summary becomes periodic with period two. In particular, each consecutive triple contains at most one \(1\), and the blocks \(U,V\) have fixed endpoint windows. Placement offsets and side lengths are affine in \(|U|,|V|\).

2. Search for cancellation of oriented edges

Take the sum of the child boundary chains and subtract the proposed parent boundary. A chain keeps an integer coefficient for each directed lattice edge. Since each interior triple contains at most one \(1\), the template for a digit is the template for \(000\) plus three possible single-bit corrections. Reindex those corrections by the position of the \(1\). Endpoint windows give explicit edge terms. Reversing a placed side uses the palindromic word and reverses its contexts.

The constant contribution along a run becomes a pair of endpoints after applying \(1-\tau_d\), where \(\tau_d\) translates an edge chain by its stride \(d\). The verifier applies the same product of these factors to every chain, one per lattice stride direction up to sign. For any fixed edge direction this operator is multiplication by a nonzero Laurent polynomial on a finitely supported integer array. The Laurent polynomial ring is an integral domain, so the operator is injective.

After these reductions every term is an explicit translated edge or a translated sum over the \(1\) positions of \(U\) or \(V\). The search chooses word decompositions that make every formal coefficient zero. That identity evaluates to zero for every admissible level. A separate check compared 130 literal seam expansions at two levels against the symbolic ledger. Moving one seam by one lattice unit left the indicated number of nonzero coefficients.

3. Certify simple boundaries

The paths within each side join consecutively. The verifier checks 46 local transitions, including the endpoint cases. Fragments separated by two or more digit positions have disjoint coordinate ranges. For nonparallel sides, cross products with their stride vectors bound all possible intersections to a fixed number of fragments near their common corner. The exact segment tests leave only the shared endpoint. Parallel sides have disjoint coordinate bands. The endpoints form a single cycle for each parent.

4. Use winding numbers to certify coverage

The verifier checks four finite base patches \(T_2,P_2,T_3,P_3\) containing 72, 124, 594 and 834 Turtles respectively. It also checks the two strip prototypes and all four ordered adjacent strip pairs. Tiles further apart in a strip have disjoint coordinate spans.

Assume the child patches are tiled with multiplicity one. At a point off all boundary edges, the sum of child winding numbers counts the child interiors containing that point. By the chain identity it equals the winding number of the parent. A point near the origin lies inside the central child, so the simple parent boundary winds positively. Its winding number is one inside and zero outside. Thus the children fill the parent and have disjoint interiors. Taking closures includes their edges. This proves the inductive step for both cluster types.

5. Exhaust the plane by nested patches

The first triangle side lies in \(x+2y\ge 2m-2\), where \(m=|p_{2n+1}|\). This follows from the bound on the finitely many local marking vertices and the fact that the stride preserves \(x+2y\). The lattice metric satisfies \((x+2y)^2\le 4(x^2+xy+y^2)\), and the other two sides are rotations. The parent therefore contains the centered disk of radius \(m-1\). The centered child of \(T_n\) is a half-turn of \(T_{n-2}\), so alternating the orientation of the odd levels gives a nested sequence. The disk radii tend to infinity, and the union is a whole-plane tiling.

A completed rectangle binds those bits at the top and yields acceptance at the bottom. An independent checker verifies the full edge matches using exact runs of transport tiles.
