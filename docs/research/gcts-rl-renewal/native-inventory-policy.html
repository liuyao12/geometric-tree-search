Propositional logic as distant markings.

\[ A,\qquad A\Rightarrow B \qquad\vdash\qquad B. \]

A modus ponens guard on line \(j\) writes the required formula \(A\) at the formula port of earlier line \(i\), writes \(A\Rightarrow B\) at the port of earlier line \(k\), and writes \(B\) at its own port. The earlier command placements write their actual formulas at those same points. Agreement connects the argument even when the lines are far apart.

\[m_{\mathrm{source}}(p_{i,r})=\operatorname{enc}(A)_r
  =m_{\mathrm{guard}}(p_{i,r}),\qquad i,k<j.\]

The encoding is canonical UTF-8 JSON followed by a distinct integer terminator. Full words are compared, not hashes or embeddings. Strict earlier references exclude circular self-support. A command-word layer couples the syntactic command to a valid guard. All original command alternatives remain in the inventory, including commands that fail to find a compatible guard.
The attention analogy applies to selecting a compatible receptor or cluster. The learned policy can score distant connections; exact point values decide whether they fit. Learned scores never establish logical validity. Formula markings define this finite proof problem. No new failure-based exclusion is learned here.
Reflexivity, axiom and generalization guards are structural instances of declared rules. Tautology guards are compiled by whole native checking of every basis formula. Unknown computation stops compilation; it does not justify pruning a guard. The finite basis consists of syntactic subformulas of the supplied assertion and axioms. There is no author-supplied proof order or reference path, but this grammar is restricted and does not cover arbitrary first-order proof search.

Matched cold controls and their costs.
Seven held-out declarations, five lanes and two fresh-process repetitions retain all seventy observations. Every lane uses the same exact-line grammar, native guard compiler and GCTS scheduler. Each has \(2000\) placement attempts and \(30\) search seconds checked before placements, except the declared zero-placement control. A proposal call can overrun the clock before the next check. The zero-policy nine-line runs therefore exceed thirty seconds. Catalog construction precedes the search clock; final native verification is a separate query.
The five lanes are base GCTS, a zero policy that pays proposal costs and defers at greedy ties, a fixed heuristic, the freshly learned frozen policy, and the same learned policy with family templates removed while arbitrary sampled clusters remain. Proposal bounds are twenty-four family clusters, \(2048\) family validation checks and eight sampled clusters with a sixty-four pair-test cap. The guard compiler permits sixty-four native queries; each native computation permits \(10^9\) selected-symbol steps.

Evidence and remaining gaps.
The independent auditor imports none of the new producer, family miner, policy or search. It reconstructs every original command and guard, scans every complete point graph, verifies dead/forced/generation decisions, exact state and rollback, every bounded proposal pool, every aggregate expansion, source justification, action probability, seeded stochastic choice, reward, gradient and weight update. It independently encodes and reruns every recorded native query with a separately written interpreter. All eight displayed full accepting response DAGs are freshly derived, and each human-readable line is decoded from its actual native context.
Three proofs supplied the newly mined inventory and five further assertions were solved during frozen evaluation. Together they contain thirty-nine native proof lines. The geometric example is symbolic incidence from given ground premises, not the full Hilbert axiom groups. Equality uses logical reflexivity and generalization, without PA axioms or induction. The high-level positional point model is searched with GCTS; the enormous primitive Wang expansion is checked using exact response acceleration, not placed square by square in this experiment.
The next substantive tests are semantic lemma reuse, certified learned failure markings, broader declarations with competitive saturation/SAT baselines, and improved learning across independent seeds. A universal correctness theorem for this finite high-level compiler and general Hilbert geometry remain open, alongside the original Turtle and Penrose program.
