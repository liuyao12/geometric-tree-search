Actual automatic search.
The rule compiler defines an authored logical calculus. It supplies source-axiom instances and generic conjunction, implication, existential and universal inference blocks. It does not supply a theorem’s proof sequence. GCTS and CSP select the inferences, proof positions and backward references from fresh finite inventories, without a prior proof, saved policy, model or coordinate input. No RL is used in this experiment.


The declared grammar recursively constructs every backward syntax production within two additional context layers and its formula-size bound. Tautologies are terminal leaves. All sorted outer instantiations and propositional clause orientations are available. Source existential elimination applies to non-implication goals whose source guard follows propositionally from the context; inner existential conjuncts of the context are also available. Existential introduction uses the formula’s binder as a free parameter. A generic context-transport option is enabled for the line/point target and disabled for the joining-line target. This is a calibrated bounded grammar, not a fair search of all first-order proofs.


Each solver receives the same inventory, cell bound and certified resource constraints for its target. GCTS maintains the complete frontier/candidate graph, checking global dead ends, global forced moves, then the earliest generation. The classical control uses arc consistency and minimum-domain branching. Search limits include point/model construction; catalog construction and proof decoding are additional costs. Total cold time also includes primitive replay and the native check. Generic native compilation and the subsequent independent audit are additional shared costs.


Single cold trials establish these particular proof discoveries. They do not establish a speed advantage. A cutoff means unknown; exhaustion applies only to the declared finite grammar. A one-cell-shorter strip and removal of the relevant axioms are included as controls. Every displayed positive proof has complete acceptance by the unchanged native checker.
