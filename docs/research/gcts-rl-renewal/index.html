Inspect all placements and proof trees ↗
Why the smallest marking is sound for complete point-model tilings

The learned scalar marking has assigned support inside the tile's positive occupancy support. Therefore every disagreement occurs between a pair represented in the complete contact catalog. The checks cover all twelve symmetry transforms and establish that every rejected contact is certified negative.

If a complete unmarked tiling contained such a pair, it would supply a legal continuation at every decision in that pair's exhaustive proof tree. Each leaf instead has a frontier point with no legal candidate. This contradiction rules out the pair. Consequently every complete unmarked point-model tiling satisfies the smallest marking.

This is a redundancy result for the stated point model, not a proof that a tiling exists. Larger marking supports can interact outside the contact catalog and remain learned restrictions until those interactions are certified.

SUBSTITUTION DISCOVERY

Motifs are a beginning.

The current library contains translation-normalized irregular sequences, proposed and selected by a policy-gradient learner. A repeated sequence is not a substitution. The missing object is a finite family of metatiles with exact expansion maps, compatible interfaces, and repeatable recursive coverage.

The dilation controls impose a fixed exterior contribution at boundary points. They only test an integer enlargement of one turtle outline. Multi-type, algebraic, and combinatorial substitutions remain open.

PENROSE · PREPARING THE DOMAIN

Exact arithmetic first.

Coordinates use \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]\), represented by four integer coefficients modulo \(1+z+z^2+z^3+z^4\). Multiplication, conjugation, and \(\varphi^2=\varphi+1\) are checked exactly.

The physical module is dense: \(\varphi^{-1}=\varphi-1\) belongs to it and its positive powers approach zero. A finite set of vertex angles cannot silently stand in for a rhomb's whole interior. A faithful point encoding or explicitly specialized control must be declared before the learner transfers.

Unmarked rhombs also admit periodic arrangements. Learning a Penrose-compatible subsystem would restrict that problem; it cannot automatically be called redundant pruning of all unmarked rhomb tilings.

TOWARD GEOMETRIC PROOF SEARCH

A computation is a tiling with a certificate.

Wang edge colors fit the shared model directly. Put \(t=1\) at a cell center and assign the four colors as \(m\)-values at edge midpoints. Admissible translations place one tile at each required center. Point agreement is exactly Wang adjacency, with no corner constraint added.

A radius-one transition rule \(f(a,b,c)\) becomes the tile

\[S=b,\qquad N=f(a,b,c),\qquad W=(a,b),\qquad E=(b,c).\]

Horizontal agreement reconstructs each neighborhood; vertical agreement checks the next configuration. The current compiler handles general finite Turing transition data, but its executable demonstration is a two-step toy machine. A separate checker verifies the computation and rejects a tampered accepting row.

Statement + formal theory→Proof enumerator + trusted checker→Accepting computation tiles→Verified proof

For a chosen recursively axiomatized theory \(\mathcal T\), compile a machine that enumerates proofs and accepts exactly when it finds a checked derivation of \(\phi\). A finite accepting rectangle would certify \(\mathcal T\vdash\phi\). This accepts provable inputs; it need not terminate on unprovable ones. A general proof checker and its compiler correctness proof are still to be implemented. Computational expressiveness does not decide every mathematical truth.

The finite accepting-computation direction differs from classical constructions where a plane tiling encodes non-halting. The latter's undecidability is background, not a certificate produced by this experiment. See the Wang computation construction and Berger's original domino result.

THE RESEARCH PROGRAM

Goals with concrete gates

  1. Implemented & checked

    Exact turtle baseline

    Complete incidence, exact rollback, global propagation, and generation-first branching. Finite patches and certificates are independently replayed.

  2. First result

    Learn geometric failure markings

    Cold contact labeling and scalar synthesis work. The smallest marking has a checked pair-exclusion soundness argument; longer-range encodings need further certificates.

  3. Pilot running

    RL proposes clusters and sequences

    Validated irregular sequences and a REINFORCE policy are implemented. Next: spatial clusters, hierarchical composition, stronger held-out evaluation, and measurable benefit after training costs.

  4. Open

    Discover a substitution

    Infer multiple metatile types and exact expansion maps. Require validated interfaces and at least three recursion levels; do not promote motif frequencies into a rule.

  5. Open

    Certify a plane construction

    Prove compatible collars and expanding coverage, or an equivalent invariant. Aperiodicity is an additional theorem. Fair activation of untouched points is still missing from growth experiments.

  6. Arithmetic checked

    Transfer to Penrose

    Close the point-model gap over \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]\), then run an unmarked discovery lane with declared translation and reflection rules.

  7. Compiler prototype

    Build checkable proof search

    Connect a real proof kernel to the computation compiler. Establish a computable statement-to-tiling reduction relative to a specified theory; evaluate search without changing proof validity.

REPRODUCIBILITY & LIMITS

Every claim has a scope.

The solver imports only the turtle's vertices and angle units from the article. Occupancy uses integer capacity \(12\); missing marking values are unconstrained, and zero is assigned. Conformance checks cover independent complete re-enumeration, shared fractional candidates, zero obligations, distant marking-only dependencies, scheduler precedence, symmetry composition, exact rollback, and rejected tampered proofs.

Growth activates newly occupied support and has no fair exhaustion of untouched \(A_2\) points yet. All growth results are finite. Learned models are frozen for each run and start anew on every cold iteration. Peak memory is measured for the whole process. Marking synthesis is an equality encoder with free values, not a neural prediction of unseen labels.
