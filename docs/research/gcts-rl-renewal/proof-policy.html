WHAT CHANGED · WHAT DID NOT PAY

Training is real. Its cost is not repaid.

Every trained policy and zero-weight policy verifies eight mathematical targets and exhausts two finite control envelopes per seed. The fixed prior does the same in seeds seven and nineteen; its first multiplication repeat reaches the wall limit and remains unknown. These are actual bounded outcomes, and the unequal-outcome fixed-prior total is not a success-matched speed ranking.

No useful RL acceleration is established. Seed seven largely learns deferral and exactly reproduces the plain search's base-attempt count. Seeds one and nineteen use almost the same work as the authored fixed hierarchy preference. Their evaluation cold times are \(10.854\), \(14.658\) and \(10.387\) seconds, while the corresponding zero-weight controls take \(10.933\), \(10.683\) and \(10.733\) seconds. Training adds \(23.185\), \(23.253\) and \(28.485\) seconds, plus shared catalog construction.

The searched fragment library remains a useful result from notebook 30. This batch adds independently replayed RL selection over all compatible bounded instances, including the option to defer. It does not show learning that consistently improves that library's ordering, discover new fragment types through RL, collapse the primitive graph into a coarse graph, or introduce new failure markings.

There are three actual stochastic training seeds and one deterministic frozen evaluation per seed and lane. The controls' repeats measure timing variation; they are not independent new theorem samples. Multiplication lies near the wall cutoff. Claims based on a tiny cold-time difference would be stronger than these data justify.

DECLARED OPTIMIZER · VERIFIED CONTINUATION RETURNS

Credit only choices that were reached.

There are twelve authored graph/interface features: deferral, deferral with multiplication, deferral with progress and degree, member count, hierarchy level, final-cell membership, direction, context depth, term shrinkage, already-placed input and member degrees. These features read the current complete graph and exact term syntax. They do not read a future proof or supplied witness sequence.

The policy samples sequentially without replacement from a softmax distribution, including a deferral action. A declared \(32\)-bit linear congruential generator exports every used uniform variate. Evaluation is deterministic with frozen weights. Floating-point preferences only order proposals; exact point checks and proof verification use the unchanged discrete values.

\[r(a)=\mathbf{1}\{\text{native acceptance and successful continuation}\}-\frac{\log(1+C(a))}{\log(1+50000)}.\]

Here \(C(a)\) counts every actual base-placement attempt in the selected proposal's validation and completed continuation subtree, or in the base fallback for deferral. The reward does not count a macro as a single placement. Rejected or exhausted proposal branches can receive negative work rewards without becoming pruning rules. A sampled suffix after an earlier successful child receives no credit. Resource-cutoff trees receive no parameter update.

\[\theta'=\Pi_{[-6,6]^{12}}\!\left(\theta+0.2\sum_a(r(a)-b_a)\nabla_\theta\log\pi_\theta(a)\right),\qquad b'=0.9b+0.1r.\]

This is a projected REINFORCE-style experiment with an authored branch-return definition and running baseline, inspired by Williams's original REINFORCE paper. No convergence or unbiased-gradient theorem is claimed for this finite pseudo-random, capped DFS procedure. Training uses base-work rewards as a proxy; the evaluation's full elapsed cost decides whether that proxy helped.

EXACT GCTS · INDEPENDENT POLICY AND PROOF REPLAY

The learner cannot certify its own proof.

The point model retains one primitive tile per cell with \(t=1\), exact scalar output and premise \(m\)-values at distant formula points, complete frontier/candidate incidence, and generation-zero finite roots. Global dead ends precede forced moves, then earliest-generation branching with degree breaking ties. Every fragment constituent is replayed through that scheduler on a copied state and graph. Rejection restores the parent exactly; every original base alternative remains.

The independent auditor imports no policy, miner, graph implementation or producer. It reconstructs every base rule and mined source window; recomputes every policy pool, feature vector, softmax probability, random draw and log-probability gradient; checks every reached-choice delayed reward, baseline and projected update; and replays every complete tree and positive primitive proof expansion. It binds all native inputs to the frozen program and original external problem.

The audit checks saved contexts and policy draws of unknown runs, but does not prove their truncated trees complete or independently replay every native instruction. Native adapter, toolchain and frozen kernel remain trusted implementation layers. This is a specialized positional arithmetic grammar with identity transforms, not a uniform four-edge Wang inventory, induction search or a general fair proof grammar.
