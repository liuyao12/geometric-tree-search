Querying a Penrose tiling by its hierarchy

Choose a region in a certified infinite Penrose tiling. The solver returns every whole rhomb that overlaps the region in positive area and derives the patch boundary from those rhombs. It uses exact cyclotomic coordinates and skips entire clusters when a separating line certifies that they miss the query.

A small window near a billion-unit offset returned 17 rhombs after visiting 229 hierarchy nodes. The enclosing supertile has 83,621,143,489,848,422,977 unit triangles by the substitution count. The search never expands that full supertile.

Use a cluster boundary to exclude all of its descendants

The earlier recovered substitution has two golden triangle types. Its certified four-step nesting map \(S\) carries the prototype \(Q\) into a larger triangle with \(Q\subset\operatorname{int}S(Q)\). The supports \(S^n(Q)\) exhaust the plane. The query chooses the first of these supports strictly containing the entire core, then follows the recovered child rules. The experiment allows up to 64 nesting rounds.

For a direction \(d\), the marking checks the ranges of the linear projection \(x\mapsto\operatorname{Im}(\overline d\,x)\). Disjoint interval interiors allow a skip, including equality at a touching edge. A certificate records one separating direction per convex component. The default fallback considers the edge normals of both polygons, which makes the test exact for positive-area intersection.

At a hit, the half-rhomb’s matching diagonal locates the whole rhomb. The audit independently descends to the companion half and checks both arrow records. Every returned tile has diameter at most two unit side lengths, so the returned patch lies within distance two of the requested core. It can therefore serve as a movable boundary inside any envelope that contains that margin.

For a fixed-size convex component, all sufficiently coarse triangles have comparable diameter and area at their respective scales. The triangles that meet it fit inside an expanded neighborhood and have disjoint interiors. Their number is bounded per coarse level. The descent to a distant window therefore adds work proportional to the nesting depth for a fixed number of bounded-size components. Exact arithmetic also uses longer integers at larger offsets.

This is a region query in one certified Penrose tiling. The query fixes the phase by its nesting seed and allows the outside tile edges to move. A prescribed seed placement, a tight outer envelope, or an exact polygonal boundary requires a different compatibility search.

What transferred between windows

A receiver recomputes both support intervals, so a learned direction can transfer across position and size. It skips a cluster only when its exact check succeeds.

Exact certificate and replay

The audit rechecked the four subdivision boundary channels, verified each query’s partition into hits and rejected subtrees, located the mate of every hit, and clipped the returned tiles to the requested core. It checked the area equality component by component and recomputed the exposed boundary. Deleting a hit, forging a root exclusion, and changing an arrow each caused rejection. A clean replay reproduced the scientific artifacts byte for byte.
