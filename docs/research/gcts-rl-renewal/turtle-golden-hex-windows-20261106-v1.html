Give each cluster a word boundary

A seam strip places its \(i\)-th Turtle at \((4i,-2i)\) in axial coordinates, with a fixed orientation and offset for each letter. The central words come from the Sturmian slope \(\alpha=(5-\sqrt{5})/10\). The fitted edge labels depend on the current letter and its two neighbors, with special symbols at the ends. Sturmian words and seam construction

Eighteen labels learned on levels one through three predicted every edge of both cluster families at levels four and five. They also agreed with the independent signed boundary sums through level nine. A one-unit shift of a side failed in every test.

Each child’s oriented boundary contributes its winding number. Exact cancellation removes shared edges. If the remaining ring is simple and positively oriented, its winding is one in the interior and zero outside. The child interiors therefore have disjoint coverage of that ring. This check uses the child boundaries and counts at every stage.
