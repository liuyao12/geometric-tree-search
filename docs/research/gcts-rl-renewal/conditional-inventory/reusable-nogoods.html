A witness must use a permanent boundary pin or a selected support placement. Missing active-domain support, parent exclusions and earlier clause filtering cannot replace primitive conflicts. The full current instance binds the certificate; its scope string is not a portable identity.

No positive performance signal from this implementation

Both original whole-workload attempts hit the deadline. The off arm recorded a prefix of \(68\) cases and the on arm \(53\), with worker high water respectively \(68.72\,\mathrm{MiB}\) and \(140.25\,\mathrm{MiB}\). The cache arm recorded \(842\) certification returns. These are incomplete-run observations, not comparable completed-bank timing scores or accepted proofs. Neither arm reached the original diagnostic phase.

Recorded inclusive graph-audit action intervals were approximately \(69.06\,\mathrm{s}\) off and \(58.87\,\mathrm{s}\) on; the latter covered fewer audits and a different prefix. Separate certification action intervals totaled approximately \(0.85\,\mathrm{s}\) on. These intervals do not isolate repeated certificate validation inside graph audits, graph copying or wrapper guards, and do not justify attributing all overhead to one cause.

A complete run would qualify this finite specialization and its actual observed certificate opportunities. It would not qualify the entire proof system, every malformed certificate or interruption site, generic mixed-generation GCTS, or a GCTS+RL advantage. The new supplemental 21-group / 28-subcase diagnostic proposal remains unexecuted and is not counted among the original 55 variants.

The direct cache is a conventional certified-constraint baseline. Any geometric or learned version should face a control with the same clauses and all certification, candidate maintenance and output costs charged. The fixed faithful first-order checker machine, its one palette, unknown-proof grammar and arithmetic/Hilbert bridges remain unfinished. This comparison discovers no new arithmetic or geometry theorem.

The next implementation question is whether the colored gadget can be compiled with exact projection checks and practical cost. The next proof-system question remains the faithful fixed checker encoding. Turtle, Penrose and practical region tiling stay in the wider program; this finite cache experiment does not settle them.
