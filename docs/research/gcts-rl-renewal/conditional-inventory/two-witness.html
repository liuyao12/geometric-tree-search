Return to larger reusable proof clusters

The declared criterion was a fresh selected target proof or an additional distinct tried alternative after a completed global decision. Neither occurred. This optimization is frozen at its tested version; further frontier/cache tuning is not the next research step.

  1. Mine larger generic cuts from already checked donor proofs, including instantiation steps and implication licenses. Give clusters adaptable term and formula receptors with explicit unresolved obligations.
  2. Expand each proposed instance into original primitive rules and check it in the current language. Use generic goal unification for proposals; supply no hand-written receiver proof or theorem-specific move sequence.
  3. Measure discovered theorems and reusable expansions against empty inventory, ground reuse and matched conventional controls, charging construction and failed proposals. Develop and evaluate a trained cluster proposer after the checked representation and baseline work.

Existing conventional controls already have checked proofs of this target. The open contribution is useful automated cluster acquisition and search, with inspectable proof lines and honest total cost. Practical region tiling, symbolic Hilbert geometry, learned markings, RL and a universal proof palette remain open program goals.
