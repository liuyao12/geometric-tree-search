What the experiment found

What was searched

These are semantic point-proof-tree searches. Every displayed primitive first-order proof and every chosen concrete family expansion receives an independent check. Families guide continuation order; they do not remove primitive candidates or add logical axioms.

This study did not search a literal Wang grid or compile these new certificates into the preceding raw NAND checker and its fixed tile palette. Square icons below depict fixed proof roles and point markings. They do not show a newly solved Wang patch or evidence that physical tiling accelerates first-order search.

The arithmetic theory is the finite addition-zero fragment, without induction. Geometry is a one-sorted symbolic incidence fragment with an uninterpreted line function. These are not full PA or Hilbert geometry. Family templates are empirical proposals, not admitted universal lemmas.
