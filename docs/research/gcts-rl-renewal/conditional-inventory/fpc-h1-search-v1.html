What would make this a GCTS contribution?

Make the reduction explicit

Define exact point values, marking domains, allowed translations and complete candidate incidence. Check global dead ends and forced moves before branching at the earliest generation. Demonstrate exact rollback and agreement with an independent verifier.

Measure learned structure

Keep the same tasks, candidate universe and budgets. Compare the base graph with proved markings, learned restrictions and validated receptor-cluster proposals. Charge learning, cluster expansion and validation costs. Expand every reusable lemma cluster to checked primitive steps.

A family of clusters can expose typed interfaces and adapt to matching receptors. That is a useful proposal mechanism; it becomes proof evidence only through its checked expansion. We still need evidence that this structure improves search beyond ordinary symbolic matching and lemma reuse.

The geometry profile is a symbolic incidence fragment with explicit point, line and incidence predicates. It does not implement full Hilbert geometry.
