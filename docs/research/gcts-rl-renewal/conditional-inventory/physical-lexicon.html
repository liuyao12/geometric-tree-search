Why this is a new completed lexical specification
The old physical routine had undefined/stuck behavior for a blank inside an incomplete byte. This successor explicitly rejects. The claimed correspondence is to the new completed specification, together with preservation of the old accepted-word language when old stuck behavior is nonaccepting. Old head positions, scan costs and partial terminal trajectories are not claimed equal.
The routine uses surrogate-preserving UTF-8, as required by the literal string carrier. It should not be read as a claim to reject every surrogate encoding permitted by that carrier.
