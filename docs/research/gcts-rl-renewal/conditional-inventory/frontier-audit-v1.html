The qualification result

All 40 completed searches passed the full point-graph audit. The two deliberately interrupted searches remained unknown. The native lane produced 12 checked proof outputs across three controls; these are repeated outputs for two schematic positive targets, not twelve different theorems.

One target derives \(G(c)\) from \(P(c)\), \(P(c)\to Q(c)\), and \(Q(c)\to G(c)\). The other derives \(\forall x\,P(c)\) from the closed hypothesis \(P(c)\). Extra unsupported and padding alternatives remain in the declared grammar. No selected derivation is supplied to the solver.

Every complete bounded proof set was preserved by literal failure markings and by the explicit filtering control. Four capacity-consistent disconnected additions were rejected as native proofs. This establishes a checked interface on the qualification cases; it does not establish a search-performance advantage.

What this reader can establish
