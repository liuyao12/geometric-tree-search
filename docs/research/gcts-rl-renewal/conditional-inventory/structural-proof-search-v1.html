What was given, what was discovered

The input is a finite map of named hypotheses, a target formula and a maximum proof length. The candidate catalogue is complete for that bounded hypothesis/modus ponens fragment. Chains, distractors, alternative paths, cycles and implications used as premises are authored controls. Renaming is reported separately from structural variation.

Two rule families, with long-range premise receptors

A hypothesis tile

At row \(i\), this tile occupies \(\mathrm{slot}_i\) and assigns its given formula \(H\) to the marking point \(\mathrm{out}_i\).

Unit occupancyOutput: \(H\)

A modus ponens tile

At row \(i\), this tile chooses earlier references \(a,b<i\). Its marks require \(A\) at \(\mathrm{out}_a\), require \(A\to B\) at \(\mathrm{out}_b\), and assign \(B\) to \(\mathrm{out}_i\).

Premise: \(A\)Implication: \(A\to B\)Output: \(B\)

The occupancy points and marking points are disjoint. A row’s own output tile and any later tile referring to it overlap at exactly the same formula-valued marking point. Their values must agree. These are anchored symbolic point tiles with extended markings; they are not a fixed literal square Wang palette.

Why the reduction is exact in this fragment

Exact coverage selects one tile per row. Induction on the row number then verifies every hypothesis and every modus ponens application. The last row is restricted to the requested goal. Conversely, modus ponens only produces subformulas of given hypotheses, so every bounded proof uses a formula in the enumerated catalogue and has a corresponding complete tiling.

Every finite row is an explicitly activated generation-zero root. The solver checks global dead ends, then global forced moves, then branches within that generation, breaking ties by candidate count and point key. All marking dependencies, including points outside the occupancy frontier, participate in candidate removal. Failed branches restore their complete state.

What this establishes, and what remains open

Outcome pending.

Conventional forward closure receives the same hypotheses, goal, line cap and search wall limit. Point search pays for its candidate representation. Independent model auditing, evidence storage and native proof checking are timed separately. Logical counters differ between implementations and are diagnostic; they are not exchangeable units of work.

The markings in this experiment define the inference rules. Their formula values are specified by the calculus; no marking learner is trained here. This isolates whether the point search can choose a proof structure from the complete rule catalogue.

The fixed native first-order checker and a separate symbolic checker validate each reported successful certificate. The proof checker does not trust the geometric search, the decoder or a status label. An unknown result is retained as unknown. A bounded exhausted proof search does not establish general mathematical unprovability.

This step addresses unknown proof structure. Reusable lemma families, learned cluster proposals, arithmetic induction, Hilbert geometry, and a fixed universal literal Wang implementation remain research objectives. No trained reinforcement-learning policy participates here.
