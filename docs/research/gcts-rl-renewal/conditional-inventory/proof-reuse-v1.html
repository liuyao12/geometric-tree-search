What this establishes

This is automatic reuse within proofs already discovered by bounded point search. It adds no new theorem or proof search, and establishes no distinctive GCTS contribution, RL result or search-speed advantage.

Arithmetic uses the addition-zero fragment without induction. Geometry here is a one-sorted symbolic incidence fragment, not full Hilbert geometry. The original proofs remain separately inspectable.
