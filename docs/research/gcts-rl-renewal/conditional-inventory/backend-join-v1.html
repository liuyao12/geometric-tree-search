The crucial distinction

A mathematical proof line is decoded from a record in the top input, together with the original signature, hypotheses and goal. A Wang row here represents one NAND gate in the proof checker. One proof record requires many gate checks, and shared syntax, substitution and acceptance checks span additional bands. There is no natural one-row-to-one-inference identification.
