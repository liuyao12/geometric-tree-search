The exact scope

The root is seeded with half of its capacity and its requested formula. A producer contributes the other half at its anchor and a half at each prescribed child, with conclusion and premise markings. Integer capacity \(2\) represents these halves. The root has generation \(0\); new obligations use the master growth convention. A rule with a child outside \(V_D=\{0,1\}^{\leq D}\) is absent. The declared comparison uses \(D=8\).

Every selected placement must touch the active frontier. This root-connected reachability premise matters: two distinct zero-rule aliases with the same formula could fill an unsupported site in an arbitrary disconnected packing. Such a first placement cannot meet the active frontier here. The decoder separately rejects disconnected extras. Completion of the reachable patch is a finite proof tree; unused sites need not be filled.

These are anchored point placements, with fixed child roles and orientation. Square glyphs are a drawing of point values, not an extra geometric legality test. This is not a single fixed translation-only Wang palette. The control duplicates repeated subproof occurrences in its output; memoization saves search work, not tree size. Tree depth, expanded tree nodes, shared DAG facts and primitive proof lines are different bounds.

All fifteen assertions were already studied in Y. They are regression and transfer cases, not globally unseen problems. The arithmetic uses finite addition axioms without PA induction. The geometry uses a symbolic incidence fragment, without a drawing, full Hilbert geometry, order, congruence or continuity. No training or RL is executed in this comparison; the old row-ID matcher is not silently reused.

What this contributes


The next family interface
A connected rooted rule patch has half capacity at its root and unfinished receptors and full capacity at completed interior sites, when its external incoming half is omitted. A growth-ready cluster learner would propose such patches with explicit shared premise conditions, validate their full base expansion against the current graph, and retain complete base fallback. That interface is a research goal, not a learned result of this comparison. The broader proof-search, turtle and Penrose program remains active.
