What did the three actuals cost?

Each cold job is counted once, including the failed training control setup. Observer elapsed samples precede final receipt output; they are not exact tool end-to-end times. Entry, compilation, search, Native, output and custody clocks overlap and must not be added as disjoint phases.

Every job retained the original limits: 160 seconds of cooperative work, 180 seconds to positive completion, 210 seconds of custody, a 225-second external fallback, 1 GiB conservative whole memory and 128 MiB physical output. Search limits remain 600 attempts and a cooperative two-second local budget. The failed control did not establish that a resource ceiling was exhausted.

What follows from this result?

The positive result is limited to fresh conventional donor proofs and their checked per-donor fragment families. The negative result is an experiment qualification failure before learning. A future study needs independently consistent control expectations and a fresh reviewed protocol; it cannot adopt a policy or evaluation prefix from this run. Choosing useful and distracting families, learning when to abstain and comparing that behavior fairly remain open.
