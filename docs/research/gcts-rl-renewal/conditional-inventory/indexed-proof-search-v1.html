Why omitting a conflict can be exact

Let \(U(p)\) be the fixed complete inventory of placements incident to a frontier point, and \(K_s(p)\subseteq U(p)\) those compatible with the current totals and assigned markings in state \(s\). During a forward extension, totals can only increase and assigned markings can only gain agreeing values. Therefore \(K_{s'}(p)\subseteq K_s(p)\). A capacity overflow or a marking disagreement cannot disappear in that extension.

This justifies delaying construction of incompatible payloads only when the indexed enumeration is complete and rollback restores candidate discovery, loaded-point certificates, dependencies and state together. All registered placements retain their full occupancy and marking dependencies, including mark-only points outside the active frontier. A rule change or weaker marking requires revalidation; the experiment freezes the inventory and markings.

The root has half capacity and its requested formula. Each rule supplies the remaining half at its output and a half at each premise site. Integer capacity \(2\) represents these values. The finite site depth is \(D=8\); placements have fixed premise roles and orientation. This is an anchored point encoding, not yet a single fixed translation-only Wang palette.

All fifteen targets and all three searches

Each point lane includes its cold provider construction and remaining engine work within a nominal two-second window. The control includes its own cold preparation and dynamic programming. Reported search times include cleanup tails, which can exceed the nominal cooperative window; this is not a hard OS lifetime guarantee. The previously audited catalogues and four-lemma bank are reused; their original preparation costs are disclosed separately.

Both point engines also have operational caps of \(10{,}000\) registered candidates, \(50{,}000\) search nodes, \(1{,}024\) active points and \(10^9\) charged work units. These are resource limits, not logical restrictions; refusal is unknown. Charged work includes validation, graph updates and canonical query/event serialization, so its operational units depend on the implementation. This is not a wall-time-only experiment.
