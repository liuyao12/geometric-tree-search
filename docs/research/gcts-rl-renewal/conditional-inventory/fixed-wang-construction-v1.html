Three targets in the named incidence fragment

A point is not a line:
\(\forall p\,(\mathrm{Pt}(p)\to\neg\mathrm{Ln}(p))\)


Distinct lines cannot share two distinct points:
\(\forall l\forall m\,((\mathrm{Ln}(l)\land\mathrm{Ln}(m)\land l\ne m)\to\neg\exists p\exists q\,(\mathrm{Pt}(p)\land\mathrm{Pt}(q)\land p\ne q\land I(p,l)\land I(q,l)\land I(p,m)\land I(q,m)))\)


A point exists off each line:
\(\forall l\,(\mathrm{Ln}(l)\to\exists p\,(\mathrm{Pt}(p)\land\neg I(p,l)))\)
