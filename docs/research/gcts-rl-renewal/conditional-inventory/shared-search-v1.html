What the pilot actually found

No GCTS proof-search advantage is established. The present contribution is a checkable reduction to a shared-fact point problem, qualified on tiny examples. Proofs found by conventional controls are attributed to those controls.

The benchmark point engine's work cap counts bookkeeping and audit serialization, as well as search operations. Reaching that cap before the wall ceiling is an unresolved attempt. Resource accounting needs calibration before adding learning or RL.

Active and formula marking layers
Each owner fills one required slot with \(t=1\). Its active and formula markings can be read at distant earlier slots. A PAD owner explicitly assigns zero active and formula values; a consumer requests active one. Several consumers may read one earlier fact without placing it again.
These are problem-defining marks, with literal agreement constraints. No learned marking, cluster bank or RL policy is used. The model is an anchored symbolic point adaptation; it is not a search on a uniform Wang grid.

Interpretation and next step
