The next contribution to test

The current program is ordinary symbolic proof search. Its point-row adapter supplies a representation with distant premise markings; it does not yet supply a complete GCTS frontier–candidate graph, global propagation, generation order, rollback, learned markings or trained RL.

A useful next direction is schematic receptor and lemma families that can be instantiated and expanded into independently checked primitive rows. This should be tested against explicit search baselines, including all construction and verification costs. The present result gives no evidence that such an inventory is already effective.

Before choosing the next implementation, collect a bounded, non-authoritative census of retained obligations, contexts, catalog entries and descriptors in a distinct declared study.
