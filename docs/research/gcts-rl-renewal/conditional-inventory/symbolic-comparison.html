Interpret the comparison carefully

This experiment isolates the search mechanism while retaining and charging the same complete point model in all arms. Symbolic search receives only logical packets and the compiler, creates no Model or Graph, then projects its candidate into the common model for checking. It therefore does not measure a standalone symbolic prover that avoids point-model construction altogether.

GCTS allows \(5{,}000\) tile-placement attempts; symbolic search allows \(5{,}000\) proof-row extensions. Those units cannot support cross-method attempt-count speed claims. All share the nominal \(30\)-second search budget, \(180\) seconds of work, \(210\) seconds through closure, a conservative \(1\) GiB sum of separate worker and native high-water observations, and a \(128\) MiB result limit. The observed peak sum is not a live aggregate allocation limit. Full native admission and final-check costs count in the cold envelope.

The schedule is hints off, fixed hints, symbolic, then its reverse. Every consumed run is retained; no failure is retried or repaired in place. Cooperative budget exhaustion is UNKNOWN, not a negative mathematical result. The historical SMT cohort remains available in the earlier quantified report; SMT was not rerun here.

Reusable clusters are present in all arms. Use of this shared lemma library is not uniquely attributable to geometry. No new RL was trained. A broader GCTS contribution still needs varied unseen assertions, stronger conventional prover controls and evidence that learned markings or cluster proposals improve costs reliably. PA induction, full Hilbert geometry, a fixed universal primitive Wang palette and practical Turtle/Penrose extension remain open.
