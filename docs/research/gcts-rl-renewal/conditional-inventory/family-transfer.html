PREDECLARED EVIDENCE

A hint must help on the proof that succeeds.

  1. Fresh proof. Search produces the exact conclusion, fills every required root and receives fresh whole native acceptance.
  2. Essential compound use. A family instance on the conclusion’s dependency ancestry binds a parameter to an implication formula.
  3. Matched hint consumption. That same ground instance occurs in a validated family hint, and a constituent of its essential call row is selected on the winning path.
  4. Changed choice. Separately record whether the preference changed the original alternative order. Selecting an already-first alternative proves consumption alone.

A family merely offered, an unused block call, or an unrelated preferred tile does not satisfy the linked criterion. Even a successful linked instance would be a small positive transfer result. It would not establish a general search advantage, useful higher-level dependency families or a trained-policy benefit. Formula and English rows, definitions, exact inputs and primitive-use costs will accompany any accepted proof.
