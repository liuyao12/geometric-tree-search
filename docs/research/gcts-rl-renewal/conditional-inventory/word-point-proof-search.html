What changed: one exact value per whole word

Original byte carrier

A formula or command occupies a vertical run of marking points: its canonical bytes followed by the terminator \(256\).

\(b_0,b_1,\ldots,b_{n-1},256\)

Checked word carrier

The same origin port receives a dictionary ID. The frozen dictionary retains every full literal word; equality uses exact bytes, with no logical normalization.

\(\operatorname{id}(w)=\operatorname{id}(v)\iff w=v\)

Preserved search problem

Candidate identities, occupancy roots, scalar mode points, certified exclusion points, initial conditions and the affected-candidate unions correspond. All Graph audits and proof checks remain.

Why compatibility is preserved: unequal words disagree at a byte, or the shorter word's terminator meets a byte of the longer word. Every word includes its origin, so replacing its byte support by that port preserves the union of affected candidates.

This is a new reduced point domain with a checked correspondence for reachable, aligned, complete-word states. It does not cover arbitrary partial or shifted byte assignments. The independent reduction verifier checks the entire declared candidate model; this is not a claim that the two representations are pointwise identical.

    How a learned failure becomes geometry
    A trial placement is excluded only after a certificate separately rechecked against the full original support shows that every original candidate at a mandatory unfilled root conflicts with that trial. A fresh auxiliary point \((-100,k)\) receives a fixed initial marking \(1\); the impossible trial receives marking \(0\) at that point. Ordinary marking agreement removes it through the graph. A work cutoff never licenses an exclusion.
    All finite required roots start at generation zero. Point search checks global dead ends, then global forced placements, then earliest-generation branching. The new carrier is checked independently against the original constructor specification, including candidate identities, internal conflicts, boundary and dependency incidence. Returned checks and search audits are distinguished from planned checks in the actual records.
    

    What can we conclude about GCTS?
    This implementation uses exact dictionary-valued marking ports. Independent checks derive the whole-word occurrences and establish their correspondence with the original complete byte-word construction. Read the method and scope.
    The fixed universal Wang construction, its full physical checking bridge, practical Turtle and Penrose tilings, symbolic Hilbert geometry, and trained RL cluster proposal learning remain separate research objectives. This development pilot cannot establish broad proof-search superiority or statistical speedup.
