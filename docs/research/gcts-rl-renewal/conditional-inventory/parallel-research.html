A shared producer can serve several receptors

The new proposer works backward from a candidate at the current genuine branch point, seeks actual rows for its missing premises, and assembles a bounded DAG. A producer is shared by its literal row and placement identities; repeated ordered ports remain distinct. It invents no formula, rule, term or admitted lemma.

A proposal becomes operative only after fresh row/port reconstruction and exact aggregate validation against the current state. Complete plans may rely on already justified selected rows. Open, depth-limited or quota-limited branches supply diagnostics; they become no logical facts or pruning rules.

The ordinary scheduler still checks global dead ends, propagates global forced moves and branches at the earliest generation. Cluster members are placed through that scheduler with exact rollback. Existing Join, arbitrary sampled clusters, defer choices and all base alternatives remain available. A final proof still needs its fresh whole-proof check.

The implementation adds a bounded proposal option to the existing shared support ordering. It is not an RL result. The eventual research claim requires better proof discovery or useful reuse under a fair budget, rather than a larger proposal log.

One bounded grouping pilot is justified

The new explicit mode starts with every original definition. If a request reaches the Native space limit and contains several target definitions, split that group at its index midpoint and try the left child first. Every child carries its complete dependency closure in original order and the full theory.

Every failed parent and every later request consumes the same aggregate \(5\times10^9\) microstep allowance. A step limit, a singleton space limit or a rejection stops certification. No accepted prefix activates a smaller search inventory.

Source review found no blocking defect in the registry/driver changes. The driver separately reconstructs the queue, full requests, dependency coverage and step accounting. This is a static result; cold-launcher coupling, runtime qualification and a new prospective declaration are still required.

Case 002 is a reasonable single engineering pilot, selected from known outcomes. Its earlier full request consumed \(872\,854\,609\) microsteps before the space stop. Repeating that probe would spend part of the new allowance, with no guarantee that the remaining groups fit. Do not multiply outcome-tuned batching variants if the pilot fails.
