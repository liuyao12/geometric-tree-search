What this contributes
Literal GCTS markings now do the candidate elimination in a checked learning loop: start unmarked, certify local finite extension outcomes, synthesize assignments, freeze them, independently prove finite preservation, then run the marked engine.
All resolved catalogue entries were used. There is no holdout or generalization claim here. Stronger receptor families and higher-order failures remain future encoding problems; they need their own preservation argument.
Cost and limits
Runs were single warm calls in fixed lane order. Acquisition, three pair labels, synthesis, exhaustive preservation, assurance and persistence cost belong to the pipeline. Tiny raw-search differences are not speed evidence.
