A reusable cluster guided a genuine quantifier proof.

All six admitted results have the same literal complete model, compiled catalog, library, ground inventory and initial state. The model has \(7{,}441\) candidate placements. Each selected proof fills all \(60\) positional points and decodes to \(12\) rows. Every proof's target-essential primitive ancestry contains \(2\) instantiation licenses, \(1\) generalization, \(3\) modus-ponens steps and the \(2\) given hypotheses.

In both fixed-guidance runs, a historical propositional modus-ponens family accepts a universal formula and an implication as its formula parameters. What changed, and what was held fixed

The earlier compiler could not instantiate these closed premises with an eligible variable. The new uniform term policy retains initial subterms whose free variables are permitted by the target and adds a variable term for every binder name mechanically collected from the original hypotheses and target, including unused binders. That pool is frozen before the two expansion rounds; later capture-renaming names and synthetic compound terms do not enlarge it. Each proposed license still needs native admission.

All methods use the complete same four historical formula-family templates, full matching and dependency closure, and fresh native inventory admission. Every exported admitted interface is preserved. Input-role arity is derived from the largest exported interface, rather than discarding larger clusters to fit a fixed field count. Internal dependency ports remain checked inside their block bodies.

Hints off retains the same blocks and primitive fallback. Fixed guidance uses the existing deterministic ranking with no new RL training. The SMT control encodes the same complete point model. The schedule is hints off, fixed, SMT, then its reverse. Literal model, compiled-language, inventory and initial-state comparisons are recorded in the summary wherever complete models are available.
