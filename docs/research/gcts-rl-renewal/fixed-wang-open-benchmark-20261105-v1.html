The block solver offers every admitted ground clause as a move. It records a failed state by its current clause and the number of inference slots left. Each inference can remove at most one literal from the current clause. A literal with no opposite in the admission inventory can never be removed. The verifier also re-enumerates the successors of exhausted states.

On the Hilbert case, the marked block search found the proof in 1,351 nodes. Plain blocks reached the 120,000-node limit with the same moves and ordering. Both SAT controls found proofs after the serial restriction was added. A block node, a boundary-bit decision and a SAT decision measure different work.
