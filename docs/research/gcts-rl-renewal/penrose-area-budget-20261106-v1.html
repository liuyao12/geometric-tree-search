Subtract the area each rhomb brings

For the requested region \(K\) and the chosen set \(S\), disjoint tile interiors give

\[ \left|K\mathbin{\triangle}\bigcup_{T\in S}T\right| = |K|-\sum_{T\in S}\bigl(2|T\cap K|-|T|\bigr). \]

For a fixed count, take the largest scores \(2|T\cap K|-|T|\). The exchange argument is the same as in the turtle, where equal tile areas made overlap alone sufficient. In the ten-rhomb example, the area-aware choice reduces disagreement by \(4-2\varphi\) thin-rhomb areas. The wide-window gain is \(-184+115\varphi\) thin-rhomb areas.
