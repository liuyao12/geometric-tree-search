Simultaneous samples and peak sums answer different questions.

Sampled simultaneous RSS

At each saved sample, sum over its unique included PID set \(U(t)\):

\(\widehat M_{\mathrm{RSS}}=\max_{t\in T}\sum_{p\in U(t)}\mathrm{RSS}_p(t)\).

The finite sample set \(T\) may miss an unsampled peak. Inclusion of workers, owners and observers must be recorded for each sample scope.

Sum of scoped peaks

For explicitly declared scopes \(\mathcal S\), sum their recorded peak values \(K_s\):

\(M_{\mathrm{sum}}=\sum_{s\in\mathcal S}K_s\).

Peaks need not occur simultaneously. A descendant-inclusive parent scope may overlap another scope; overlapping values are not distinct-process peaks.

With consistent units, complete coverage and disjoint scopes, \(\max_t\sum_s M_s(t)\leq\sum_s\max_t M_s(t)\). This is an envelope comparison, not an equality of measurement methods. Scope overlap, missing observers, reserve and unobserved intervals need separate labels.

Saved platform documentation and bounded calibration address the units and descendant semantics of APIs such as resource.getrusage, RUSAGE_SELF and RUSAGE_CHILDREN.
