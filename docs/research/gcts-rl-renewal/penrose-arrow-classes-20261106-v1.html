Two sides of the same length need different marks

The inputs are two unmarked golden triangles, ten rotation directions, reflections, and inflation by \(\varphi\). Coordinates are exact in \(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_5]\). The independent search starts at an extreme corner and grows through full shared edges. It found two acute partitions and three obtuse partitions.

In each acute partition, the equal parent sides have different refinement profiles. One becomes a single edge of length \(\varphi\). The other becomes edges of lengths \(1\) and \(\varphi^{-1}\). Reversing directions preserves the number of edges in a profile. A single unit receptor would require the same profile on both sides, so at least two types are necessary in this edge-to-edge rule model.
