Why the highest overlaps are optimal

Let \(K\) be the request and \(S\) a set of \(n\) whole tiles from the fixed plane tiling. Write \(A\) for one turtle’s area and \(w(T)=|T\cap K|\). Since tile interiors are disjoint,

\[ \left|K\mathbin{\triangle}\bigcup_{T\in S}T\right| =|K|+nA-2\sum_{T\in S}w(T). \]

With \(n\) fixed, exchanging a selected tile for an omitted tile of greater overlap improves the objective. A certificate therefore needs the count and a cutoff. For the wide window, the smallest selected overlap was \(\frac{2}{5}\) of a tile’s area and the largest omitted overlap was \(\frac{2}{5}\). Tiles beyond the containing supertile have zero overlap, because its proved inscribed disk contains the request.
