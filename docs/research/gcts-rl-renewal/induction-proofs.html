REAL PROGRESS · THE CLASSICAL CONTROL STILL MATTERS

Two induction proofs found by GCTS.

The point search receives arithmetic axioms, the target and finite bounds. It receives no proof sequence, selected induction variable, previous proof, policy or cluster library. The authored grammar offers induction on every quantified variable in the target, ordinary axiom rewrites in both directions at every bounded occurrence, and rewrites under the fixed induction hypothesis. A compiled inference can expand to many primitive logical lines; its proof rule is supplied, while its choice and connections are searched.

Certified resource markings improve these small GCTS searches. A marking can reach a distant premise cell and reject a candidate there through ordinary point-value disagreement. This is a concrete contribution to automated proof search and an inspectable geometric encoding of a necessary proof-resource condition. The classical AC/MRV control remains faster on the positive cases. These are exploratory single runs with rotated lane order; they do not establish a general speedup. No new RL or learned failure marking is claimed.

The primary track remains Wang-style proof search. Next, discover reusable conditional fragments and let a fresh RL policy propose their compositions, with a classical control receiving the same fragments. The larger two-variable successor statement remains a useful open test. Euclid-style constructions, a uniform finite Wang inventory, unrestricted mathematical expressiveness, practical movable boundaries and certified infinite tilings remain research goals.

A REDUNDANT CONSTRAINT · INSPECTABLE SOUNDNESS ARGUMENT
Why the resource markings preserve proofs.


For every formula \(F\), form its full backward dependency cone \(C(F)\), including all syntactically available inference rules and cycles. An actual proof ancestor of \(F\) must have a formula in \(C(F)\). If premise cones are pairwise disjoint, their ancestral cell sets cannot overlap. Otherwise, their union has at least the size of the largest premise ancestor set.


\[b(F)=\min_{r:\,\operatorname{out}(r)=F}\left(1+\begin{cases}\sum_{P\in\operatorname{in}(r)}b(P),&\text{pairwise disjoint premise cones},\\\max_{P\in\operatorname{in}(r)}b(P),&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}\right)\]


Seeds have bound \(1\); formulas without any finite derivation remain unreachable. The empty-input maximum is \(0\). At the fixed point, every rule bounds its output by one plus the applicable premise bound. Induction along any actual proof's acyclic cell dependencies shows that \(b(F)\) is no greater than its distinct ancestral cell count. A formula in zero-based slot \(j\) therefore requires \(b(F)\le j+1\). This is a sound lower bound, not generally the exact minimum proof size.


Every output and every remote premise contact writes \(m=1\) at \((2j,4)\) when its formula is unreachable or \(b(F)>j+1\), and \(m=0\) otherwise. Fixed boundary markings require \(m=0\) at all these points. All original candidate types remain present; agreement removes the impossible contacts through the complete candidate graph. Every original exact solution satisfies the added zeros. There is no runtime pair classifier or supplied proof path.


A weaker control uses least dependency height \(d(F)\), with seeds at \(0\) and each inference adding one to the maximum premise height. It writes a separate output mark at \((2j,3)\) when \(d(F)>j\). The ancestral-cell bound can add independent branches, while height counts only the longest path. Shared premises and repeated formula references must not be added; the tests include those counterexamples.


INDEPENDENT INVENTORY · EVERY EXECUTED PREFIX · NATIVE BINDINGS
Keep the claimed result within its evidence.


The independent auditor imports no search, graph, grammar generator or producer. It reconstructs all bounded terms and every applicable axiom/hypothesis occurrence, verifies all induction-variable alternatives and compiled definitions, calculates dependency heights by synchronous layers and resource cones by separate backward traversals, and re-enumerates every point graph at every decided search node. It audits complete and open trees, scheduler choices, placement attempts, classical support removals and decoded command sequences. All accepted proofs independently expand to primitive logical lines. Native program/input bindings are checked; native instruction execution, the compiler and the arithmetic theory remain trusted layers.


The point engine uses exact integer twelfths, scalar marking agreement, complete incidence, global dead ends before forced moves, then earliest generation with candidate count breaking ties. All finite roots have generation zero. Rollback copies the exact state and graph. Tiny independent solution-set and graph tests confirm the resource marking invariant. No broader infinite-tiling, general proof-language completeness or asymptotic search result follows from these finite experiments.
