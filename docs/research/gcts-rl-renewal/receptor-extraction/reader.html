What this contributes—and what remains to test.


This gives concrete, provenance-preserving candidate lemma bodies obtained from discovered proofs. Their ports encode the exact formulas required for reuse. A recipient may choose new legal physical addresses around its available receptors; it must still validate the entire primitive expansion and all shared tile identities.


The extractor recognizes a declared pattern: one equality-substitution instance followed by two applications of modus ponens, acting on either argument of the incidence predicate. It does not learn the pattern language. This is automatic normalization and extraction within that fixed language, rather than evidence of a learned GCTS or RL advantage.


No recipient search uses this candidate library in this experiment. It therefore establishes neither faster search nor an advantage over ordinary lemma caching. Admission as ordering advice, receiver controls, matched baseline and ordinary-library comparisons, held-out statements and learned proposal policies are subsequent stages.
