      What this establishes
      
    
      
    
      This Source successor reuses a checked state only in the immediate witness loop of one synchronous decision. Ordinary placement, cursors, replay and independent degree checks keep their fresh paths. Each candidate still undergoes a full prototype rebuild, point-legality check, ordered read-obligation check, exact packet comparison and collision checks.
      
    
      The router uses the independently checked finite-fibre protocol for assigned non-implication outputs and preserves the legacy path for implication or missing outputs. A complete zero or singleton census can justify a local dead end or forced move. Two witnesses establish only a lower bound.
      
    
      The intended retention protocol preserves its entire historical receipt and census. Candidate packets separately reflect current read occupancy. The original fallback stream continues to determine canonical branch order; a fresh complete cover must agree with the entire actual cursor survivor set.
      
    
      These controls use declared conditional patches. Exhausting one such patch does not prove that the original arithmetic target is impossible. The interrupted-child control exercises restoration with an explicitly declared interruption; it is not evidence of a native timeout or an exhausted child.
      
    
      The current tests do not deliberately mutate a live context or establish protection against arbitrary reentrant callbacks. Context lifetime and binding also require Source inspection. Post-placement decisions and rollback snapshots are distinct checks; there is no extra post-rollback decision in the transfer fixtures.
      
    
      The previous global control suite remains incomplete. This report does not establish a new target theorem, a complete universal Wang palette, RL learning, full GCTS conformance or a speedup. Autonomous target discovery and a matched ordinary-search comparison remain necessary.
