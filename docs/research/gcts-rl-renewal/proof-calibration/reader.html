Read each automatically found interpretation.

The language is unsorted first-order logic. \(\operatorname{Point}\), \(\operatorname{Line}\) and \(\operatorname{Incident}\) are ordinary predicates. Every quantified variable ranges over the entire four-object domain. Different constant names may denote the same object.

The only nonlogical axiom here is the guarded uniqueness fragment of Hilbert's incidence axiom:

\[\forall P\,\forall Q\,\forall l\,\forall m\;\bigl(H(P,Q,l,m)\to l=m\bigr).\]

Here \(H\) contains the two point predicates, two line predicates, \(P\ne Q\), and all four incidences. The notation \(H^{-}\) omits only the distinctness clause. This experiment does not include the remaining Hilbert axioms, such as existence, order or congruence.
