Finite certificate scope

The sampled atom carrier is periodic on \(A_2\). Its boundary directions are multiples of \(30^\circ\). After a global rotation, an admissible lattice similarity is an integer multiple of \(1\) or \(1+\omega\), with \(\omega=\exp(i\pi/3)\). Their area ratios are \(k^2\) and \(3k^2\). The two-cluster screen uses these same nine representatives and the declared edge-connected pair library.

These certificates rule out particular finite substitutions. A plane construction would require a recursive family of exact partitions and a separate argument that its iterates cover arbitrarily large regions. Larger clusters and other inflation scales remain open.
