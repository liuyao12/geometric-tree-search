Finite certificates

The three-tile bank enumerates every way to extend an edge-connected pair by a third Turtle, then identifies identical supports under translations and the twelve lattice point actions. A legal connection shares a segment of positive length. Of the 8,036 candidate extensions, 5,454 have disjoint interiors. They reduce to 2,241 distinct supports.

Each negative tree queries one required cell per node. Every legal alignment at that cell must appear as a child. A leaf has no legal alignment. The checker can replay the complete tree by absolute alignment enumeration without reading the learned bank. A partition into larger tiles from this library would also partition into primitives, so the primitive contradictions rule out a closed substitution within this bank at these scales.

Reusing one proof at arbitrarily large multipliers

The finite proof can sometimes move with a cluster vertex. In sample coordinates, write a tested point as \(k s v+p\), where \(v\) is the anchor vertex in the base shape and \(s=18\). The side of each scaled supporting line with normal \(n\) and base constant \(b\) is given by \(k s(n\cdot v-b)+n\cdot p\). Bounds on the second term give an exact multiplier beyond which the side stays constant for every atom the proof might inspect. The verifier then checks the translated proof at that multiplier and separately checks any intervening multipliers.

When the learned markings save time

The bank contains 168 patterns learned from earlier two-tile failures. Each pattern records three to five unavailable cells whose relative offsets intersect all 240 primitive alignments of a queried cell. An exact check can independently reproduce the rejected domain.

On the 4,482 smaller inflations, full bounding-box enumeration used 27.6 times the root-check CPU of a whole-grid marking pass with exact fallback. That enumeration constructs every contained placement. An early-exit baseline stops as soon as it finds one dead cell and reuses the covered sites of every legal placement it discovers.
