What the comparison measures

Each method had the same primitive scheduler and limits of four search seconds, 1,200 visits, depth 96 and 32,000 cached occupancy fields. Method time includes guard setup, initialization and search. Assurance and shared replay of the donor certificates are reported separately. Cut removals count search events and may recur after rollback.

The six qualification runs matched exhaustive subset enumeration on the same two placements. The guard retains the second placement when its certificate queries a point outside the target. It rejects the pair when the entire footprint is required. A separate fixture checked that an installed cut removes every affected candidate incidence and that rollback restores the graph exactly.

The first cohort had one run per method per region. The larger cohort was frozen after the first, with the same search limits. Unknown jobs give bounded search prefixes. The evidence here concerns finite point obligations and certificate pruning.
