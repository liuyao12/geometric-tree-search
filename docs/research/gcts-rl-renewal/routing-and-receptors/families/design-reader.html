Markings and proof checking have distinct jobs

Translation-only point markings encode exact compatibility between complete members and interfaces. They do not certify formulas. A proposed family cannot remove base candidates or justify degree zero or one. Any new elimination marking needs its own scoped redundancy proof or an explicitly restricted experiment.

Local primitive membership and a compatible pending cluster are not a complete theorem proof. The full expanded root remains subject to the original native verifier. Finite admitted instances do not establish universal schema soundness.
