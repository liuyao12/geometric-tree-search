Why the search could discard a clause

If the instantiated literals of an earlier clause are a subset of a candidate's literals, the earlier clause entails the candidate. The search indexes earlier clauses by a signed predicate and a rigid term address. It tests up to 48 donors of at most two literals per candidate. A goal-supported candidate requires a goal-supported donor. The matcher checks every deletion's substitution, sorts and literal inclusion.
