What the program has established, and what comes next.

GCTS bookkeeping and stochastic proposals can drive automatically chosen legal moves, while an independent checker reconstructs the actual arithmetic trajectories. Primitive geometry domains can be represented exactly without materializing every decorated identity. These are concrete implementation results. They do not yet demonstrate improved proof search or a universal proof tile set.

The next goals are to implement and check whole-cluster invalidation/waiting/rollback, establish primitive-to-logical/native certificate correspondence, then run fresh geometry discovery with a frozen budget. Arithmetic needs broader, harder and held-out tasks with complete replay and matched baseline costs. Learned receptor families must retain their checked primitive expansions and complete fallback.

The research goal remains active. Finite unknown runs do not establish unprovability. Source-level valid authored proofs do not establish search discovery. Aperiodicity and universal computation do not substitute for an implemented sound proof encoding.
