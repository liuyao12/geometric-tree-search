Propositional inference can become a distant marking interface.

A formula is first a symbolic word. A line exposes that word at output receptors; an inference placement reaches back to the premise receptors it uses. For modus ponens, the required earlier words and the later conclusion are

\[P,\quad P\to Q\quad\vdash\quad Q.\]

The same literal formula characters meet at distant points. A compatible placement therefore checks both the chosen rule and its premise identities. These dependencies need not lie on adjacent square edges. The earlier mathematical proof reader already shows such symbolic, long-range inference interfaces; the native computation rectangles below test the fixed finite palette underneath that approach.

The transformer analogy applies to choosing which earlier lines to inspect. Attention scores can propose useful references or clusters; they cannot turn a high score into a valid inference. An inference's premise equality is part of the proof certificate. A separate learned search marking needs certification before it may exclude candidates; otherwise it can only guide ordering with complete original fallback.

There are thus two useful layers: logical dependency markings, which the checker must validate, and redundant search markings, which can be erased after discovery. This experiment exercises the second layer through certified projections of the original Wang values. It adds no learned attention or RL training.

A complete factored domain replaces an impossible tile list.
The fixed alphabet has \(60\) tape symbols and \(462276\) states. A configuration symbol is ordinary or carries a state and its underlying tape symbol. A tile is identified by its three input symbols, with at most one head. Its original south and north values are the center input and local successor; its west and east values are overlapping input pairs.

\[S=b,\qquad N=F(a,b,c),\qquad W=(a,b),\qquad E=(b,c).\]

Four disjoint finite products cover the ordinary, center-head, left-head and right-head cases. Their exact total is \(264143617200\) original tile types. Count queries inspect the pinned transition table; lazy pagination retains every original candidate. The oracle receives integer receptors and allowed symbol sets, with no theorem name, formula evaluator, supplied proof sequence or proof callback.


The additional projection \(\pi\) removes the state while retaining the underlying tape symbol:

\[\pi(s)=s,\qquad \pi(\operatorname{head}(q,s))=s.\]

Projected tiles assign these values at input, output and neighboring input points on a separate layer. The fixed single-head input also certifies a write cone: at configuration row \(k\), an initially ordinary cell at position \(x\) keeps its underlying symbol whenever \(\lvert x-h\rvert\geq k\), where \(h\) is the initial head position. The head moves at most one cell per step, and an ordinary center receives either its old symbol or a head carrying that same symbol. Guarded complete rows preserve the single head. Every original complete rectangle therefore admits all these additional values. Removing them leaves a certificate in the unchanged original palette.


This is certified constraint synthesis from machine semantics. It does not learn the markings from failed branches. The neighbor method copies exact input values to more distant points; the projected method additionally uses the certified cone boundary. Their differing behavior measures the combined methods, not an isolated effect of the cone alone.


The markings change native search; an overall speed advantage is not established.
Six fixtures, three methods and three balanced fresh-process observations give \(54\) cold solves. The two-row inverse fixture falls from \(193\) placements to \(10\), with medians about \(1.473\) and \(1.269\) seconds. The three-row case falls from \(391\) to \(202\) placements but rises from about \(2.189\) to \(7.288\) seconds. Candidate counting and propagation can cost more than the avoided placements.
The actual boot prefix stays unresolved at \(2000\) placements in base search; both extra-marking methods fill it in \(68\). That prefix reads no assertion or proof payload. A too-early accepting top is a finite exhaustion. The reject-center control checks that a global dead end outranks other forced cells; a zero budget remains unknown. No negative result here establishes mathematical unprovability.
