Costs and independent audit

Fresh certificate acceptances are independently replayed before subsequent reuse. A prior acceptance is reused only through the byte-identical request, complete input bands and the earlier 142-query full replay receipt. New models are reconstructed from every full-word candidate, then every new and paired learned tree is replayed with complete global dead/forced/earliest-generation decisions, proposal pools, fallback and rollback.

The historical common preparation is not rerun, but its full cost stays visible. The historical bill below is the previous complete geometry-family milestone. Earlier donor discovery, receptor promotion and policy training are additional inherited costs, linked through its frozen plan and earlier reports; they are not rerun or included in this incremental bill. Production and audit overlap on one host; their elapsed times are separate task costs and should not be added as sequential wall time. Certification elapsed time also includes waiting for independent replay before fresh acceptance reuse. Search timings were collected in separate milestones, so this is not a controlled total-cost speed claim.
