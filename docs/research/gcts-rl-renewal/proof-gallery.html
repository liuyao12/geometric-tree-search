NEXT CENTRAL EXPERIMENT · DESIGN, NOT A COMPLETED ENGINE

Search over semantic proof tiles.

Concentrate on Wang-style tiles with GCTS's more general finite point support. Keep Turtle and Penrose as regression and occasional geometric controls. Revisit them when they answer a specific question about proof interfaces, hierarchy or distant dependencies.

Illustrative proposal. Distant read points connect to the exact formula values assigned by earlier constituents. A completed implementation must track these incidences in both directions.

A rule tile occupies its proof cell with \(t=1\). Its scalar \(m\)-values specify exact syntax IDs at its conclusion and premise points. For modus ponens, a candidate binds \(P\), \(P\Rightarrow Q\) and \(Q\); their shared values may lie beyond adjacent cells. Finite candidate envelopes enumerate the valid bindings and finite offsets.

Term formation, substitution, quantifier scope and eigenvariable restrictions need explicit interfaces and expansion checks. A learned lemma tile exposes its premises and conclusion and expands into a checked lower-level derivation. A failed partial proof may justify a reusable marking only after a complete failure certificate or a proved invariant.

  1. Direct cold discovery. Withhold every derivation and theorem-specific skeleton. Let GCTS choose rule instances, earlier references and lemma composition under the fixed statement and axioms.
  2. A controlled comparison. Hold the proof grammar and finite envelopes fixed. Compare ordinary symbolic search, adjacency-only encoding, distant GCTS markings, then RL proposals and discovered lemma tiles. Preserve every base alternative.
  3. Charge the whole pipeline. Include syntax/catalog construction, graph initialization, search, learned interfaces, expansion and verification. Measure completion rate as well as time, proof size and memory. Test new statements, not only training assertions.
  4. Increase expressive coverage. Grow syntax and reference envelopes fairly. Every tile family has finite support at each stage. No arbitrary truth oracle, unbounded-radius fixed tile or complete negative decision is assumed.

Global dead ends and forced moves must precede branching at the earliest frontier generation. Distant support does not waive complete bipartite bookkeeping, exact rollback or the distinction between proved pruning and a learned hypothesis. The proposed semantic engine must record conformance before it can support a GCTS performance claim.
