What this gate establishes.

All \(171\) conditional source bodies are independently checked and recursively expanded. Their local assumptions become receptor aliases with no new occupancy. Every primitive witness belongs to the frozen recipient relation. Formula codes, context, theory, authority, source maps, ordered references, vertical translation and exact point values determine full placement identities.

Whole-union validation checks unit occupancy and every assigned marking, including distant points. Missing markings and assigned zero remain distinct. Only equal full primitive keys may share occupancy. Fixed priorities are independently reconstructed and bound into the whole pool authority epoch.

This gate does not enumerate the complete live candidate graph, audit scheduler decisions or establish search exhaustion. It does not demonstrate new lemma discovery, a universal native Wang compilation theorem, an unrestricted first-order calculus, learned failure markings or an RL advantage. The original source-body proofs are existing infrastructure; generating compatible bindings is the new work.

The \(17\) corruption controls modify individual group payloads, recompute their self-hashes and compare them to independently rebuilt groups. They are not complete altered-file or owner replays. Earlier synthetic fixtures remain separately scoped and charged. Exact pinned standard JSON inputs are the parser scope.
