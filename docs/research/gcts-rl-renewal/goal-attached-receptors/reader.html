What would succeed, or falsify the idea

Require an actual initially pending receptor later discharged by automatically found primitives, followed by the independent closed-target certificate. Compare checked outcomes, inclusive native cost, peak memory, recipe visits, branches, forced moves, backtracks, lawful matches, embedding failures, discharged obligations and expanded word length. Normal primitive markings may eliminate candidates; the family itself is advice, not a new proved pruning rule.

Negative controls must reject dangling-as-fact, circular/future-row or swapped-port proposals, capture/arity errors, stale epochs, excluded keys, assigned-zero conflicts and shared-key overfill. Check dead/forced precedence and exact graph-plus-agenda rollback. No matching recipe/embedding means no advice, not impossibility.

No viable match, no additional checked success and no inclusive cost reduction would fail this pilot. If primitive-only backward search produces the same gain, the family did not establish a contribution. If ordinary benefits equally or more, there is no distinct GCTS advantage. Right-one replay alone is training validation. A small recipient pilot cannot establish universal transfer, full PA, induction, native Wang compilation or RL.
