Reuse a failure across branches

At the gold site, every one of the 240 primitive poses that fits the outline overlaps at least one of the 8 committed tiles shown. The next branch matched all 8 placements after placing 68 tiles. The remaining tiles in that prefix are irrelevant to this conflict.

A more general mark can also use previously certified conflicts. For a sample \(q\) and a set \(C\) of committed placements, consider every in-bounds tile that could cover \(q\). Each option either overlaps \(C\) or extends \(C\) to a previously refuted set. The checker then certifies the clause \(\bigvee_{t\in C}\neg T_t\), where \(T_t\) means the cover uses pose \(t\).

If the clause holds before the most recent decision, the solver returns it past that decision and skips the remaining sibling branches. The inventory indexes small clauses by pose and checks whether the current branch contains the whole set. The 597-tile run stored 343 such sets of at most ten placements and used them to close 137 branches. This pilot stores placements in outline coordinates.
