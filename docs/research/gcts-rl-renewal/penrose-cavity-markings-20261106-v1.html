Check the area of each sealed component

Work with thin and thick unit rhombs in \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]\), with edge-to-edge contacts. Let \(\phi=(1+\sqrt{5})/2\) and take the thin tile area \(\sin(\pi/5)\) as the unit. A finite patch must have area \(m+n\phi\) with \(m,n\) nonnegative integers. The area coefficients are unique because \(\phi\) is irrational.

A sealed regular pentagon has twice-area coordinates \((1,3)\) in the basis \((1,\phi)\). A concave pentagon has \((1,1)\). Either pair rules out a filling by whole rhombs. Two separated regular pockets have summed area \(1+3\phi\), so a global area test permits that sum. Each component must nevertheless be filled separately.

Each exposed pocket edge has a placed rhomb immediately outside it. Any new tile whose interior crosses the pocket boundary would overlap one of those rhombs. The fully required pocket must therefore be covered by whole tiles that lie inside it.

The face walk follows outgoing unit rays in angular order. It recovers a bounded cycle even at vertices where the exterior and cavity boundaries touch. Enumeration found 724 supporting five-rhomb frames for the regular pocket and 168 for the concave pocket. The verifier checks each selected loop against the actual exposed tile edges and recomputes its area independently.

The ring calculation and contact model use the two rhomb shapes. The marked substitution and its plane certificate are checked in the separate Penrose hierarchy notebook. Penrose hierarchy · De Bruijn’s algebraic treatment

Read the marking on a search branch

For a fixed target, the verifier checks that the whole pocket lies inside the required region. For a movable boundary, it checks containment in a required convex core piece. The mark is an oriented ring and an exact area certificate. The bank begins empty, learns a ring on its first certified rejection, and recognizes translated and rotated copies in later requests.

Compare the same search with three cavity policies

The baseline uses whole-target area where applicable and the existing contact checks. The fresh arm checks every eligible cavity directly. The learned arm uses the same exact check and reuses an earlier certificate when the ring matches. The six nearly closed requests begin with a specified local frontier edge so that all three arms examine the sealing action. The remaining requests use the inherited search ordering.
