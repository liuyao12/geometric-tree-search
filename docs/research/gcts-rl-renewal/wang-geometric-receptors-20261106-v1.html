Assign placements to two blocker groups

Write \(R\) for the obligation to cover a receptor. A Penrose receptor requires one rhomb along a directed edge. A Turtle receptor requires one tile covering a point atom. The exact source checker enumerates the legal placements and assigns each to a group \(G_i\) whose corresponding blocker \(X_i\) excludes it.

For the unrestricted Penrose edge, the two groups have three placements and one placement. In the Turtle tests, every required atom has 240 lattice placements. A second enumeration from exact polygon bounds and point-in-polygon tests agrees with the indexed atom inventory. In each context there is a placement restored by dropping either blocker alone. Reversing the Penrose edge gives a checked positive control.

The Wang rectangle receives the resulting clauses. The geometry admission supplies the complete placement sets and checks the binding of the group symbols. The rectangle checks the logical derivation from those clauses.
