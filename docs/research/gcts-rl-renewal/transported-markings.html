A SUFFICIENT CONDITION, WITH A SMALL PROOF
Why a closed prefix can be restricted.
Every cell assigns one of the continuation transitions \((1,1)\), \((1,0)\), or \((0,0)\). The fixed exterior values are \(\phi_0=1\) and \(\phi_{B+1}=0\). There is exactly one end, and continuation never returns from \(0\) to \(1\).
An end seed at position \(j\) fixes the end to \(j\). A padding seed at position \(j\) forces the end to satisfy \(e<j\). For every transferred pair, this gives \(e\leq B_0\), where \(B_0\) is its certified donor bound.
Suppose a recipient solution with \(B\geq B_0\) contained that pair. Remove all cells beyond \(B_0\). They are padding, and the cut still has continuation value \(0\). All required donor cells retain full capacity. Every inference reference points backwards inside the retained prefix.
Invert the checked bijection of function and predicate names. Variables, their scope values, arities, axioms, hypotheses and grammar rounds are preserved. The complete formula and rule catalogs are independently checked to correspond. Whole formula words are reserialized; their lengths may change. This is a symbol transport of the point encoding, not an allowed spatial rotation or a rigid motion of the encoded word.
The cropped, inverse-renamed region would be a valid donor solution containing the original pair. Its independently exhausted search tree proves that no such solution exists. Thus the transported pair is impossible and its disagreement marking is redundant for every recipient bound satisfying these conditions.
This is a mathematical solution-map argument whose hypotheses the checker enforces. It is not a universal compiler soundness theorem formalized inside the arithmetic proof kernel. Complete word ports, unit occupancy, backwards references and the monotone continuation layer are essential assumptions.


A finite failure can become a successful larger proof.
The same two universal-instantiation tiles cannot finish the requested conclusion in a two-inference region. In a four-inference region, the search adds modus ponens and generalization and succeeds. Neither seed fixes an end or padding boundary. The checker rejects this transfer. Finite exhaustion alone cannot justify a reusable exclusion in a larger region.
