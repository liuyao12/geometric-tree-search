Keep the evidence, improve the experiment


  1. Preserve the complete adviser return. The first harness saved only the UNKNOWN eligibility reason, not its partial batch, work counters or reason field. A reviewed diagnostic revision is prospective and unactivated in these runs. The cause of this UNKNOWN remains unmeasured.
  2. Make proposal construction cheaper. Source inspection shows eager construction of many future generalization demands before the streams emit advice. A smaller generic demand/round policy is an option to test, with no claim that it will fit or succeed. The original fallback and full mathematical inventory must remain available.
  3. Qualify the actual consumer, then compare discovery. Require every essential consumer case, including genuine nonempty pending-family rollback and continuation, without skips. Only then run fresh canonical and advised arms on the same target and count all costs. Learned receptor/lemma families and RL come after this measured baseline.


Clusters may eventually provide reusable lemmas with primitive expansions and typed interfaces. Here that remains the design goal; no learned lemma inventory was exercised.
