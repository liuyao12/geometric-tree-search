This is not enumeration of every possible pair of rows. Every resolved result is retained; unknown teachers authorize no marking.

    
Any placement saving must be assessed against collection, admission, validation and propagation costs. Benefits from reusing a certified layer across searches are separate from creating it. This small study can motivate a larger benchmark; it cannot establish a general advantage over other proof search methods.

    
Learning forbidden combinations is established constraint-recording research, including Dechter's Learning While Searching in Constraint-Satisfaction-Problems. The question here is whether encoding those constraints as point values, together with adaptable lemma clusters and a learned policy, is useful. A later comparison should include a direct nogood-learning control with the same learned constraints; this study does not demonstrate a new principle of constraint learning.
