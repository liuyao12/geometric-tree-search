Exact model and scope

Each point is an integer tuple in the basis \((1,\zeta_5,\zeta_5^2,\zeta_5^3)\) of \(\mathbb Z[\zeta_5]\). The ten rays are powers of \(-\zeta_5^3\). For a ray \(u\), the signed projection of a point \(v\) is represented by \(\operatorname{Im}(\bar u v)/\sin(\pi/5)\), a value in \(\mathbb Z[\varphi]\). Order comparisons use integer signs and squares. The diagrams use floating-point projection.

A region here is a prescribed finite set of saturated corner sites. The outside is free, and all rhombs must meet edge-to-edge where they touch. A completed witness covers the neighborhood of every target vertex. Classical matching rules, plane existence, and substitution are separate questions.
