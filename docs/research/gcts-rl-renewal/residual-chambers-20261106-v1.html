Split and reuse residual tiling chambers

A tile can separate a fixed region into smaller regions. The solver can reuse an exact local cover or an exhaustion proof when the same chamber appears at another position.

Split the remainder and check the join

Subtract each placed tile’s oriented boundary from the target boundary. A split is admitted when the remaining edges form disjoint counterclockwise simple cycles whose boundaries do not touch. Any tile that fits inside the remainder then belongs to one chamber.

Translate each chamber to a canonical origin. Its inventory record holds either a complete tiling or a refutation that exhausts every tile at a chosen exposed edge. A local refutation is valid even when the surrounding tiles impose additional contacts. For a cover, check every proposed tile against the original boundary and neighboring tiles. If the join fails, continue the ordinary search at that state.

Turtle uses the allowed poses in the axial \(A_2\) lattice model. Penrose uses the two unmarked unit rhomb shapes over \(\mathbb{Z}[\zeta_5]\), with full-side internal contacts. These are finite fixed-boundary results under those placement rules.

Propose a separating tile from the boundary

In a second comparison, each held-out outline has an empty initial patch and no nominated edge. The scan arm looks for tiles that touch the boundary in multiple arcs. The receptor arm learns two separated exterior contacts from an earlier bridge. It binds the first contact to the current boundary, tests for the second at its stored offset, and generates the indicated tile pose.

Both proposal arms check that the tile fits, that its placement separates chambers, and that at least one chamber matches the inventory. They try the resulting tile first and retain every placement at the selected edge.

Replay the covers and refutations

Every admissible completion puts a tile along the selected segment of the exposed boundary. That segment anchors a finite catalogue of poses even in the Penrose coordinate module. Each placement uses positive area, so a bounded region limits the depth of an exhaustive proof.

The verifier reconstructs the boundary at every certified branch, regenerates that edge’s placement catalogue, and checks conflicts with exact clipping and contact tests. It rejects a refutation with a missing placement or an incorrectly translated chamber.
