What the certificate proves

Let \(V\) be the original formula-label registry and \(K\subseteq V\) the supplied membership bit vector. The independent checker retains the whole original relation and verifies:

\[\begin{aligned}\operatorname{out}(r)&\in K &&\text{for every zero-input recipe }r,\\ \bigl(\forall p\in\operatorname{in}(r),\;p\in K\bigr)&\Longrightarrow\operatorname{out}(r)\in K &&\text{for every original recipe }r,\\ G&\notin K.\end{aligned}\]

Here \(G\) denotes the formula label for the original target; its registry code is \(186\). The actual checker scanned all \(16{,}201\) relations and rejected a certificate whose target bit was set.

\(K\): \(5{,}896\) membersClosed membership; not a derivation for each member.
\(V\setminus K\): \(5{,}253\) labelsThe original target code \(186\) is excluded.

Widths show membership counts, not registry order. Every formula derivable through these recipes must lie inside the blue set. The blue set may also contain unreachable labels: the checker does not establish leastness or a derivation for each member.

The induction is short

  1. A first primitive step has no prior inputs, so its output is in \(K\).
  2. If all strictly prior inputs of a later step lie in \(K\), the checked relation places that step’s output in \(K\). Repeated ordered premise ports are retained.
  3. Induction over any finite recipe word keeps every output in \(K\). Since the target is excluded, no such word reaches it.

This covers any finite word length in this exact Horn relaxation, hence also the original \(24\)-row limit. It does not assert that the unrestricted independent word checker, a broader grammar or first-order arithmetic lacks a proof. It does not certify a point-search trajectory or complete board.

A deliberate distinction. The \(5{,}480\) figure counts zero-input recipe records. Different records can have the same output. The \(5{,}896\) members are a checked closed set, not individually certified reachable formulas; subtracting the first number from the second is not a count of proved derived facts.

The Source explanation: terms exist only where the grammar licenses them

The earlier Source diagnosis and its distinct logical review explained the obstruction before this actual certificate. The current compiler freezes the equality-operand pool before its two instantiation rounds. It retains terms up to \(3\) nodes plus permitted target/axiom subterms, and only identity or successor hole contexts with another frozen operand.

\[\begin{gathered}P=\{0,n,S(0),S(n),S(S(0)),S(S(n)),\\0+0,0+n,n+0,n+n,A\},\qquad A=n+S(S(0)),\\B=n+S(0)\notin P.\end{gathered}\]

The addition-to-successor term missing from this frozen pool can later occur inside an instantiation formula without becoming an equality operand. Missing one attractive family binding alone would not exclude every alternative proof; the diagnosis therefore also checked the other possible routes.
