Method and controls

The calculus uses lifted resolution and factoring on equality-free clauses. A signed predicate/arity index offers complementary literals, and exact unification determines which pairs can resolve. A clause can be promoted by a score on a bounded shortlist. Regular FIFO slots keep the rest of the queue moving. The checker replays the recorded parent indices, substitutions and clauses through the empty clause.

The policy started from zero weights on 30 training cases. A separate 12-case split chose the fixed control and learned seed before evaluation. Input families in the synthetic splits are distinct. The study used 3 interleaved repetitions and a cap of 180 clause activations in each evaluation run. A case counts at a deadline when a majority of repetitions return a verified proof within it.

Time includes search, scoring and verification from parsed clauses. The total sums per-case medians, including unsuccessful runs. Counted work sums clause activations, candidate pairs inspected and unification equations processed. The time and work include bounded misses. A bounded miss leaves the conjecture open.

The saved-data audit replayed 407 proofs and checked 6 finite countermodels. It found 1 checked proof among the 180 guarded-relay training episodes. Verification and training by family.

The six external clause sets come from the TPTP v10.0.0 Problem Library. TPTP Problem Library.
