Searching for proofs with Wang tape fragments

The fixed Wang verifier now takes part in search. Each proposed resolution move gets a short fragment with two previously derived parent clauses on its boundary. An admitted fragment can extend the proof inventory. A rejected fragment can teach a marking that applies to later moves.

On Hilbert incidence uniqueness, a fresh marking inventory skipped 778 of 1,163 tape checks and preserved the proof. Counting parent checkpoints and the cost of replaying the rejected fragments used to learn the marks, the tally of implied tape transitions fell by 59.1%.

One learned marking on the tape

The machine stops at a fixed state after it verifies a proof line. The search records a checkpoint after loading the two parents. The recorded read footprint ends before the candidate suffix, so the same checkpoint can serve candidates with different text and widths. A successful candidate stops at the next proof-line checkpoint. A missing pivot reaches a rejection state after the scan of the right parent.

From that rejection, the learner records one exclusion for each literal of the right parent. A literal can have the wrong sign, or a term node at a named address can disagree on symbol or arity. Every exclusion has to hold before the marking can skip a later tape check. The term address serves as a receptor. Its physical column can move when a new parent or pivot has a different length.

What the learned ranking changed

The first ranker is a contextual bandit trained on 8 generated problems. It made 227 updates using tape-checked move outcomes. Its features include parent clause sizes and how many immediate term heads align with the pivot. During evaluation the ranker was frozen. It changed some inference orderings, with 1,492 proposals across the evaluation compared with 1,492 for plain ordering. Hilbert incidence uniqueness took 1,163 proposals.

The first scheduler finishes each group of candidates before expanding a newly derived clause. A second test lets a new clause offer moves immediately to a shared frontier. Both frontier arms use learned failure marks. The learned priority arm trains on a separate set of generated episodes with discounted credit for reaching the empty clause. Its evaluation policy is frozen.
