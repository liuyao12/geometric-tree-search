A component needed before proof-tile search

The original arithmetic axioms, target, bounded six-rule language and fixed \(24\)-row point model are unchanged. These are deliberately constructed control states, with no saved proof word, donor proof or lemma library as input. They test candidate evidence rather than theorem discovery.

Two witnesses give a lower bound

Two distinct, fully legal candidate placements establish \(\deg(p)\ge2\). The checker reconstructs their primitive instances, complete point values, remote markings, full identities and current exclusions. This does not require an exact count.

Zero and one require coverage

When the output formula is assigned, its outer constructor is not implication, and every earlier output mark is assigned, the checker can enumerate every matching original axiom, hypothesis occurrence, generalization occurrence and ordered modus-ponens pair. Only a completed census supports \(\deg(p)=0\) or \(\deg(p)=1\).

Instantiation and equality-substitution tiles produce implication licences, so they cannot supply a non-implication output. Missing marks or other uncovered cases remain unknown unless two legal witnesses are checked.

What this establishes for GCTS


This is a checked local interface, not an integrated GCTS proof search. The remaining work is a complete implicit frontier/candidate relation with reverse marking dependencies and fallback, global dead-end checks before forced moves, earliest-generation branching, and exact rollback. No GCTS or RL advantage follows from these controls.


Exclusion fixtures assume a particular branch context. They test whether exact excluded keys are respected; they do not prove that those exclusions may be learned or applied globally. Unavailable or unreachable cases are recorded separately from passed controls.


The proposer, independent checker and control harness have different authors. The coordinator reviewed the checker and harness, owned the measured activation and reused the existing native meter and cold-run helpers.
