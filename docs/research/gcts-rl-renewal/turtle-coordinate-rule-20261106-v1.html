Transport the whole-plane certificate

The earlier certificate checks the Golden Hex recurrence in its own lattice frame. Let \(F_C\) be the frame change for a child of type \(C\), and let \(F_B\) be the change for its parent \(B\). An address with turn \(G\) and translation \(v\) becomes

\[ \bigl(F_B G F_C^{-1},\,F_B v\bigr). \]

The checker compares the constant term and both length coefficients of every transformed translation. It checks the turns modulo the proved symmetries of each child. The base patches and the strip stencil agree under the same frame changes. It also checks that applying the claimed parent symmetry to the list of child addresses preserves the list modulo those child symmetries, so the symmetry argument propagates from the bases.

The learned and reference rules therefore produce the same patches up to their fixed frame changes at every level. The earlier proof establishes exact seam cancellation and simple boundaries for the reference recurrence. It also gives centered disks of unbounded radius in the odd triangles. The center cap of \(T_{2k+1}\) is a half-turned \(T_{2k-1}\), so alternating half-turns of the odd triangles form a nested cover of the plane. Read the seam and topology certificate
