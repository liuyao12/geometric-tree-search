Free shapes identify rotations and reflections. Enumeration includes every edge-connected shape at each listed size, then excludes holes. Periodic results have exact lattice certificates. Unresolved includes finite-depth survivors and budget cutoffs; it never means non-tiling. The histogram uses the convention allowing outer holes.

A successful finite patch proves \(H\geq k\), not an infinite tiling. An impossibility proof at the next depth establishes \(H=k\). We track \(H_c\) (outer holes forbidden) and \(H_h\) (outer holes allowed) separately. The reproduced record benchmarks have equal values under both conventions.
Method, scope and limitations

The search uses exact integer cell occupancy, all lattice rotations and reflections, complete point–placement incidence, and full vertex-contact surrounds. All choices of the inner coronas are reconsidered at each depth. SAT witnesses are checked by independent vertex-sector counts; upper-bound proofs are checked with DRAT-trim. This is a specialized CDCL experiment, not the reference GCTS generation scheduler.

Blind hole-free searches add explicit enclosure cuts when necessary. No published corona or learned marking is supplied to the solver. Small periodic searches use at most two copies in a period cell; failure in that family is inconclusive. Runs may overlap and their times are diagnostic, not comparative benchmarks.

The broader polygon record \(H=6\), from Bojan Bašić, lies outside these three regular-grid polyform families and is not reproduced here. This experiment establishes lattice results; it does not independently exclude off-grid Euclidean placements.
