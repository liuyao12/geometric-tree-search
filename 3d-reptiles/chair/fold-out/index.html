THE BUILD

Two printed pieces. One axle.

  1. Print the housing and rotor. The STL files are in millimetres, with the axle axis vertical for slicing. Check supports at the upper housing shoulder and bearing ears.
  2. Place the rotor in the open corner bay. Align its hubs with the housing bearings.
  3. Slide a straight \(2\,\mathrm{mm}\) axle through the bearings and both rotor hubs. The nominal length is \(44\,\mathrm{mm}\). A printable pin is included for a fit trial.
  4. Turn from seated to folded. After checking the motion, a small amount of adhesive at the axle joints can lock the final position. Keep adhesive away from the rotating hubs until then.

The nominal radial body clearance is \(0.30\,\mathrm{mm}\), with \(0.30\,\mathrm{mm}\) at each end of the rotor. Printer fit has not been tested; try this coupon before making a whole chair.
